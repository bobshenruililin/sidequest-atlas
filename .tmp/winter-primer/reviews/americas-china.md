### 2.3 EDB SSE / SSEBR (short-term, 2026-27 page)

[Government Subsidy for Short-term Exchange Activities](https://intlaffairs.hku.hk/government-subsidy-for-short-term-study-abroad-programme) — heading **“in 2026-27.”** Eligible programme period **1 Sep 2026 – 31 Aug 2027**.

- **SSE** and **means-tested SSEBR:** local UG + SFO means-tested grant/loan or CSSA family.
- **Non-means-tested SSEBR:** local UG; lifetime cap HK$18,000; once.
- SSE: once, irrespective of amount.
- Same destination cannot be subsidised twice across these schemes.
- Apply within 1 month after the activity. Physical activities need **pre-approval by an HKU organising unit**.
- Amount = destination + duration (then × SFO % for SSE / means-tested SSEBR, with a 50% floor).

### Melbourne — January (Deakin)

BOM **Melbourne Olympic Park** (Wikipedia; **2013–2025**, not a 30-year normal — shorter series): Jan mean max **26.8 °C**, mean **21.7 °C**, min **16.5 °C**, rainfall **57.6 mm**. ### Blacksburg, Virginia — January (VT)

NOAA 1991–2020 (Wikipedia, Town of Blacksburg): Jan mean max **41.6 °F (5.3 °C)**, mean **31.9 °F (−0.1 °C)**, min **22.1 °F (−5.5 °C)**; precip **3.23 in (82 mm)**; snowfall **7.7 in (20 cm)**; ~3.9 snowy days. Appalachian plateau winter. Campus in session-break mode for much of late December; residential winter classes, if the catalog split holds, start **4 Jan**. A visiting student without a dorm contract is in a small college town in the cold. Roanoke–Blacksburg airport is the named commercial field (Wikipedia / town page).

### Santiago — January (TFAS)

DMC 1991–2020, **Quinta Normal** (Wikipedia weatherbox citing [climatologia.meteochile.gob.cl](https://climatologia.meteochile.gob.cl/)): Jan mean max **30.4 °C**, mean **22.0 °C**, min **13.6 °C**. Mediterranean **dry summer** — rainfall at the annual floor. Hot days, cool nights, Andes visible on clear ones (texture, not an itinerary). Providencia in January is a working capital in holiday season, not a winter school streetscape. Valparaíso day trip is on the TFAS page; do not add unlisted vineyards or ski areas (ski is the *other* hemisphere’s winter, i.e. Chilean July).
