## 5. City essays — what two winter weeks feel like

Climate figures are **normals**, not a 2026–27 forecast. Day-length figures are **calculated 2026 sunrise–sunset totals** (Tutiempo; they state weather is not included). Sunshine hours are **observed monthly means** from national services via Wikipedia climate tables. No current events.

### Shanghai — late December / early January (family city)

Shanghai sits on the Yangtze estuary’s alluvial plain, the Huangpu cutting Puxi from Pudong. Humid subtropical (Köppen Cfa). CMA 1991–2020 normals at Xujiahui: January mean 5.4 °C, maximum 8.7 °C, minimum 2.9 °C, about 72 mm of rain on 10.6 days, 2.1 snowy days, 114.3 hours of measured sunshine — a few bright hours, not a snow-globe. Siberian northwesterlies can freeze nights; they do not make this Harbin. Calculated January 2026 daylight is about **10 h 20 min** a day. Winter, on the CMA seasonal division, is already in from early December.

Two weeks in the LINK-S window (27 Dec–16 Jan) are not a first encounter with China. They are a return to grandparents, a kitchen, a dialect in the stairwell. The river is the same working river; damp sits in unheated corridors; daylight ends while the family evening is starting. The course asks for about 100 on-site hours plus a demo day — a project inside a household, not a backpacker loop. Meaning, if it comes, is how the project sits beside an ordinary winter. Memory is already stored here. Do not overwrite it with “immersion” copy.

*(CMA 1991–2020 via Wikipedia “Shanghai”; LINK-S dates: tl.hku.hk/link; day length: Tutiempo Shanghai 2026.)*

### Seoul — late December (daylight, cold, campus winter schools)

Seoul is a Han River basin city under a dry-winter continental regime. Korea Meteorological Administration 1991–2020 normals: January mean **−2.0 °C**, mean maximum 2.1 °C, mean minimum −5.5 °C, only 16.8 mm of precipitation, **7.1 snowy days**, and **169.6 hours** of sunshine — cold, dry, and often clear. The Siberian High sends north-westerlies; the Wikipedia climate page, citing KMA, still records the old local lore that the bitterest days arrive in roughly three-day pulses. Calculated January daylight is 305 h 32 min, about **9 h 51 min** a day; December is shorter still (298 h 51 min for the month).

Two winter weeks on a Seoul campus therefore feel like early dark and hard air, then surprisingly bright noon on dry pavement. Yonsei’s Winter Abroad calendar for 2026–27 puts classes **22 Dec 2026 – 8 Jan 2027** (Christmas and 1 Jan off; extra class 2 Jan). Korea University International Winter Campus publishes a 3-week strand **22 Dec 2026 – 8 Jan 2027** and a 4-week strand through **18 Jan 2027**. Those are lecture-and-dorm products in a megacity winter, not a folk-high-school house. They collide with LINK-S almost day for day. Hanyang International Winter School is on the IAO list; its homepage this access did not yield a 2026–27 date block — treat HIWS dates as **unverified** until the host page is re-read.

*(KMA 1991–2020; Yonsei winter.yonsei.ac.kr calendar; KU winter2.korea.ac.kr; Tutiempo Seoul 2026.)*

### Vilnius — early January (Baltic dark, language school)

Vilnius is a humid-continental capital (Dfb) in a river-bend of brick Baroque and a UNESCO historic centre. Lithuanian Hydrometeorological Service normals: January mean about **−3.7 °C**, mean maximum −1.5 °C, mean minimum −6 °C, ~48 mm precipitation on **21.7 days**, humidity 88%, and only **35.1 hours** of measured sunshine in the month — a little more than an hour of actual sun on an average January day. Calculated January daylight is 243 h 15 min, about **7 h 51 min**; 1 January 2026 is listed at 7 h 09 min on Tutiempo’s Vilnius calendar. Lakes around the city are described as almost always frozen from December to March.

Vilnius University’s Faculty of Philology **has published** a two-week Lithuanian Language Winter Course: **4–15 January 2027**, 50 academic hours, classes at Universiteto g. 5 from 09:00, often until 15:00, then cultural lectures and weekday guided tours (Old Town, Palace of the Grand Dukes, KGB Museum named). Price **650 EUR**; 3 ECTS; registration/payment until 20 Dec 2026 if seats remain. Two weeks here are grammar in the morning and Baltic dusk by mid-afternoon: meaning is linguistic and historical; memory is dark courtyards and frozen river light, not a residential East–West house. The course overlaps LINK-S’s second half. Winter-course students are **not** eligible for a national D visa via VU mediation — a logistics fact, not a fare.

*(LHMS / Wikipedia Vilnius climate table; VU flf.vu.lt winter course; Tutiempo Vilnius 2026.)*

### Lille / northern France — January (dates published)

Lille sits on the Deûle in French Flanders, a temperate oceanic climate (Lesquin 1991–2020): January mean **4.1 °C**, mean maximum 6.6 °C, mean minimum 1.7 °C, 58.2 mm rain on 11.2 days, 4.2 snowy days, and only **62.2 hours** of sunshine. Calculated January daylight 262 h 55 min, about **8 h 29 min**; 6 January 2026 is 8 h 08 min, 23 January 8 h 43 min. Winter here is grey and wet more often than it is white. Averages stay just above freezing; records have reached −19.5 °C.

Université Catholique de Lille **has published LILLE Winter 2027: 6 January to 23 January 2027**. Registration opens 1 September 2026; application and payment due 15 November 2026. The page calls it a 2-to-3-week programme modelled on the European Summer Program, 4 to 8 ECTS, morning and afternoon course options (French language; project management; art and cinema; museums; teams; a sustainability option “to be confirmed soon”), plus a 3-hour intercultural module. Two January weeks in Lille feel like canal light at 16:30 and a packaged European studies product. It overlaps the end of LINK-S. Package **price is not quoted as a single NOK/EUR figure on the fetched page**; do not invent one.

## 6. Memory, meaning, experience — 3 winter weeks vs a 9-day academy

Use the three words as **jobs**, not moods.

**Experience** is what the body and the calendar actually do. LINK-S: ~240 hours across a semester of lectures plus **100 hours on-site** in a 21-day Shanghai block, inside a family city, in ~10-hour January days, with a project and a demo day. NEWDAY: **9 days**, lectures + dialogue groups + two writers’ houses + walks + a contingent campfire, in **17-hour** Lillehammer summer. Experience scales with duration, light, housing form (family / hotel / dorm / academy internat), and whether meals are shared. A 3-week winter school in Seoul or Blacksburg can out-experience NEWDAY in hours and still under-experience it in cohabitation.

**Memory** is what remains as a scene after the hours dissolve. Nine residential days are short enough that almost every meal is a scene; the campfire and Bjerkebæk are designed to stick. Three winter weeks can produce fewer scenes per day once a commute exists. Shanghai is the special case: memory is **already there** (grandparents). A LINK-S week that only restates the family archive is wasted. Berlin is the other special case: WHS 2025 already occupies the memory slot; a January HUWISU week would be a different, thinner film on the same city. Memory is not a reason to duplicate a city.

**Meaning** is the thesis the traveler can defend later — compounding, not a caption. LINK-S meaning, if earned, is institutional and familial at once: 12 transcript credits that **do not enter GPA**, a named subsidy, a project in a Mainland hub, beside a household that is not “the field.” NEWDAY meaning is form: East–West dialogue as a practice, Hong Kong used as a translator, no credits, no GPA, a fee that buys the house. A language school in Vilnius means “I spent two dark weeks on Lithuanian.” A Yonsei winter campus means “I took for-credit classes in a cold, bright city.” Those are real and smaller.

**Operational comparison (winter 2026–27 window):**

| | LINK-S / 3 winter weeks | NEWDAY / 9-day academy |
|---|---|---|
| Calendar (published) | 27 Dec 2026 – 16 Jan 2027, after HKU assessment 7–23 Dec, before Sem 2 on 18 Jan 2027 | 29 Jul – 6 Aug **2026** done; **2027 unpublished** |
| Light | ~10 h Shanghai; ~6–8 h if swapped for Baltic/Nordic winter | ~17 h Lillehammer summer |
| Housing form | not specified as academy internat on LINK pages; for Bob, family city | Nansen internat, meals included in NOK price |
| Academic object | 12 credits, pass/fail, excluded from GPA/honours | no HKU credit |
| Collision | occupies the whole IAO winter-school season | summer; no winter twin |
| Failure mode | treating family as discovery; collecting a second conference-shaped week | inventing 2027; calling every winter school “the next NEWDAY” |

Choose experience when the gap is hours, climate, or a language. Choose memory when a scene does not yet exist. Choose meaning when a form will still be true in a year. NEWDAY’s gold standard is meaning-through-form in a surplus of light. LINK-S’s gold standard is meaning-through-duration in a city that is already home. Most IAO winter rows are experience without either gold standard. Watch 2027 NEWDAY; do not invent it.
