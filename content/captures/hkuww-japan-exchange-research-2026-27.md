# HKU Worldwide → Japan exchange research notes (2026/27–2027/28)

**Compiled:** 2026-08-19  
**Scope:** Outgoing HKUWW placements at eight Japanese partners. Sources are official HKU IAO pages, host-university exchange pages, and host fact sheets/PDFs linked from IAO where available.  
**Rule:** Quotes below are verbatim from sources unless marked *paraphrase*. No invented dates, fees, or requirements.

## HKU Worldwide — global layer (all partners)

### Programme hub
- **HKUWW overview:** https://intlaffairs.hku.hk/hku-worldwide-student-exchange

### HKU IAO eligibility (applies before host-specific screening)
> "Non-final year students: those enrolling in the second-to-last or last semester in the current year are NOT allowed to apply"
> "Year 2 or above students will be given higher placement priority than Year 1 students"
> "Not joined any previous semester exchange programmes or special visiting programmes organised by IAO, faculties or departments"
> "students with a CGPA of 3.0 or above have a relatively higher chance of getting a placement in the programme"
> "an overall band of 6.5 or better in IELTS Academic Module in the same attempt"
> "a score of 93 or better in the TOEFL internet-based test/ TOEFL iBT Home Edition in the same attempt"
> "English language requirement – which could be higher than IAO’s requirements" *(host institutions)*

**Programme fee (HKU side):** All eight IAO programme pages list **"HKU Tuition Fees"** (i.e. continuing HKU tuition while abroad; host tuition typically waived under exchange MOU).

**Assessment:** CGPA (Semester 1 in application year), personal statement (100 words), English proficiency, interview if applicable.

**Deposit on acceptance:** HK$2,000 (refundable subject to conditions on HKUWW page).

### Duration (host)
From HKU-linked fact sheet / USTEP Type U:
> Period of Study: "1 semester" or "2 semesters (full year)" — Autumn admission October–March (1 sem) or October–September (2 sem); Spring admission April–September (1 sem) or April–March (2 sem).

**HKU vs host tension:** IAO lists **Full year**; UTokyo explicitly allows **one semester** on Type U. Confirm quota/placement length with IAO after offer.

### Restrictions (host Type U — key quotes)
> "First- and second-year undergraduate students are not accepted for USTEP. For students enrolled in three-year undergraduate programs, they must have finished at least 1.5 years of the program at the time of their arrival at UTokyo."
> English courses: "A minimum TOEFL iBT score of 90, IELTS overall band score of 6.5, Cambridge English Qualifications \"C1 Advanced\", or Duolingo English Test (DET) score of 120"
> Japanese courses: "JLPT Level N1" (or Japanese-medium high school exemption paths per site)
> "Type U students are not allowed to participate in practical work (e.g. laboratory work, design studio work etc.)"
> "Over 300 English-taught courses are offered each year, spanning the humanities, social sciences, and STEM fields"

**USTEP nomination/application deadlines (fact sheet):**
- Autumn admission: nomination **February 14**; application **February 28/29**; results by early May
- Spring admission: nomination **September 16**; application **September 30**; results by early December

### English-taught incoming path
- **USTEP Type U** (primary HKUWW path for undergraduates)
- **Global Liberal Arts (GLA)** courses in English (SDG themes incl. health): https://globe.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/globalliberalarts.html

### Tuition wedge (degree-seeking — national rate)
Source: https://www.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/prospective-students/tuition_fees.html

| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Admission fee | ¥282,000 |
| Undergraduate tuition (annual) | **¥642,960** *(enrolled April 2025 onward)* |
| | ¥535,800 *(enrolled 2024 or earlier)* |

**Exchange stay:** Type U page / fact sheet: exchange students **exempt** from examination, admission, and tuition fees at UTokyo.

**Exchange living funds (visa):**
> "120,000 JPY per month" — "One semester: 720,000 JPY / Full Year: 1,440,000 JPY"

**Housing:** Fact sheet: "Most students have been successfully accommodated in the university housing" but "housing cannot be guaranteed."

### Public health / medicine / social science access
- **Social science:** Strong English access via Type U catalogue + GLA (health, poverty, diversity themes quoted on USTEP page).
- **Public health / medicine:** No general Faculty of Medicine clinical placement for Type U; **no laboratory/practical work**. Access is **course-catalogue-limited** — must verify annual Type U course list (published ~late March for following AY).
- **Uncertainties:** Whether specific public-health-adjacent Faculty courses appear in a given year's Type U catalogue is **year-specific**; not confirmed in static pages.
