## How to spend attention (not a spray)

**This week (before term):**

1. HKU Portal → Online Applications → Application for Awards → **General Round (UG Year 2)**. Screenshot the deadline. Submit one serious form, not nine schemes from a brochure.
2. Email AAS scholarships desk (address on the AAS contact page) + GHD: *are you nominating for Sir Edward Youde Memorial Scholarships (local UG) this cycle, and can a Year-2 GHD with CGPA 4.09 be put forward?* Youde is the rare prestigious award that **explicitly stacks**.
3. Read the Tung & Ngai award letter. If concurrent awards are banned, General Round wins that pay tuition/living may have to be declined. That is compliance, not humility.

**Semester 1 later:**

4. **I&T Scholarship** (~late Oct / early Nov): apply only if you can name one InnoTech Area (likely Life & Health Technology or Sustainability and Green Technology) **and** a budgeted attachment that does not smash Laidlaw LiA summer 2027. CS minor + heat-health is not automatically I&T. A scraper with no product is twinning Nicholas, not I&T.
5. **HSBC Hong Kong:** skip unless need is still true.
6. Do not apply AIA / Chen / JC / Evergreen / Social Work. The window or the faculty is wrong.

**False bargains**

- Treating the nine names as a Year-2 to-do list.
- “Offer they cannot refuse” packets to JC/Chen after the Year-1 gate.
- Using Tung & Ngai *and* chasing another tuition scholarship as identity.
- Youde **overseas** on 4 Sep because the word Youde appeared.
- Semester at Sea as enrichment theatre.
- I&T as a prestige sticker on a public-health CV with no artefact.

**Steal:** JC/Chen/T&N all want intellect + character + service + (for Chen/T&N) China/HK return. You already passed that exam. Convert it into **one heat-health artefact** and **one General Round form**, not a second scholar identity.
