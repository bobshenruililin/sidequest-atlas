## What this hub actually is

CEDARS Campus Life lists project funds it administers, plus pointers to **IAO**, **Horizons**, **China Vision**, and admissions scholarships. The enrichment lines that CEDARS itself runs are **Hsin Chong** (multi-university student projects) and **Yang Tuck Ming** (local students, service in Hong Kong or abroad **excluding Chinese Mainland**). Law Sun sits on the [Financial Subsidies](https://www.cedars.hku.hk/ssi/societiesactivities/financial) sibling page, not on the Educational Funds index.

None of these mint a named scholar title comparable to Tung and Ngai, Laidlaw, Martin, or Youde.

## Live / calendar-tied (the only real scout)

### 1. Government subsidy for short-term activities, 2026–27

[IAO short-term page](https://intlaffairs.hku.hk/government-subsidy-for-short-term-study-abroad-programme), accessed 24 Aug 2026.

Three schemes: **SSE** (means-tested), **SSEBR** (means-tested, listed Belt and Road destinations only), **non-means-tested SSEBR** (local UG, listed B&R destinations, lifetime cap **HK$18,000**).

- Eligible activities **1 Sep 2026 – 31 Aug 2027**.
- Apply **within 1 month after completion**.
- AY **2025–26 leftover**: all applications by **15 Sep 2026**, regardless of activity end date.
- The application link on the page is still labelled **for AY 2025–26**. Do **not** use that Qualtrics for an October 2026 trip. Wait for the 2026–27 form.
- Duration = **official programme commencement to completion**. Travel days do not count.
- Duration bands (HK$): SSE has **no** 5–13 day band (starts **14–28 days** at **6,000**). Means-tested SSEBR / non-means SSEBR **do** have **5–13 days** (**3,500** / **1,050**).
- Destination add-on exists (Europe SSE **8,000**; non-means SSEBR Europe **2,400**; Central & South America SSE **15,000** / non-means **4,500**). **SSEBR numbers only if the destination is on the listed B&R table.** Spain, United Kingdom, Mexico, Colombia: **unverified** against that table. Do not promise.
- Priority to students who have **not** already received government-funded subsidy. University **reduces** the amount for actual costs and **other subsidy/sponsorship**.
- Means-tested SSE / SSEBR need SFO assistance or family CSSA. Operator General Round paste said **no TSFS/UFA** — treat means-tested doors as **closed** unless that was wrong.
- Conferences need HKU endorsement to represent the institution.

**GEST Valencia / Laidlaw London (Oct 2026)** sit in AY 2026–27. A **3-day** public conference can fail the **5-day** SSEBR floor and **will** fail SSE’s **14-day** floor. Do not claim GEST as a 14-day exchange. LiA ~6 weeks would sit in the **29–90 day** band **if** the pairing and destination list check out — pairing (Medellín / Mexico City) remains **unverified**.

Stacking: Tung and Ngai overseas subsidy is a **maximum**, Laidlaw LiA travel is already in play, government subsidy is extra **only if** eligible and receipts are declared. Do not double-count the same airfare.

### 4. China Vision — Non-means-tested Mainland Experience Scheme

[MES page](https://www.chinavision.hku.hk/non-means-tested-mainland-experienc).

- Local **and** non-local full-time UG. **Once** per undergraduate, irrespective of amount.
- Mainland activity ≥ **3 days**, substantive learning, **organised/coordinated by an HKU unit**, **1 Sep 2025 – 31 Aug 2026** on the current page.
- Reimbursement within **30 calendar days** after the activity ends. Rolling, first-come-first-served.
- Amount = destination subsidy + duration subsidy; HKU may cut for actual costs and other sponsorship. Example on page: 95 days → **HK$8,400**. Do not quote that as Bob’s number.
- Gannan (Mar 2026) and Wu Zhi Qiao (May–Jun 2026) are **almost certainly past the 30-day clock**. Do not file them now.
- Winter **LINK 2026/27** (Shanghai *or* HCMC) is **after** 31 Aug 2026. **Do not assume** the 2025–26 form covers it. If destination is **HCMC**, MES does not apply (not Mainland). If **Shanghai** and HKU-organised, wait for a **posted 2026–27 window**.
