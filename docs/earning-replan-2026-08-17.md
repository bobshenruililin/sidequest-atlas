## 0. Verdict in one page

The 24 July plan was not made stale by drift. It was **never executable**, and one of its core mechanics is now verified to be illegal-if-done-casually.

Three findings change the shape of everything:

1. **The immigration gate that would have killed self-employment does not bind him.** Tung & Ngai eligibility requires "permanent residency with the right of abode in Hong Kong" ([T&N eligibility](https://tungngaifoundation.org/eligibility/)), and T&N was awarded. Non-local students are barred from "establish or join in any business" and that bar was **not** lifted by the 1 Nov 2024 part-time-work exemption ([ImmD guidebook ID(E)996 ¶20(b)(ii), ¶23](https://www.immd.gov.hk/pdforms/ID(E)996.pdf)). As a permanent resident he is outside that regime. Confirm once, then stop re-litigating. **Inference, high confidence — not read off his documents.**
2. **"Hire two friends for a Saturday move" is dead.** Under Employees' Compensation Ordinance s.40, an employer must have EC insurance for **every** employee — part-time, temporary, one-day, no exceptions — with minimum cover **not less than $100 million per event** for employers of ≤200 people; failure is prosecutable to a maximum fine of **$100,000 and two years' imprisonment** ([Labour Dept concise guide](https://www.labour.gov.hk/eng/public/ecd/pco360.pdf), [ECO FAQ](https://www.labour.gov.hk/eng/faq/cap282a_whole.htm)). The July design already put transport with insured movers. It must now also put **labour** with insured movers. Bob supplies coordination and documentation, and employs nobody.
3. **The residual business is a paperwork product, not a labour product** — and it is remote-deliverable, automatable, and sellable as a template. That is the only version that survives a Y2 calendar where October, winter, summer 2027, and summer 2028 are already sold.

And the blunt frame: he is asking how to earn money while holding full tuition + HK$40,000/year living support (public [T&N benefits](https://tungngaifoundation.org/financial-awards-benefits/)) plus family capital. The marginal HKD from a student moving service is small; the marginal attention cost in Y2 is large. So this is not a cash problem. It is a **non-scholarship-dependent identity** problem plus an **operating-experience** problem. Rank the ideas by *skill and asset residue per hour*, not by HKD per hour. Canon already says abundance is measured as optionality rate — cash that buys no new option is a bad trade even when it clears.

## 1. Blunt correction of the 24 July plan

**Correction 1 — it was calendar-illegal at birth, not "stale".** A "48-hour weekend launch" of a physical, local, in-person, weekend-dependent service was scheduled for someone who was about to be roughly 8,000 km away for a month, then walk into a term start. Calling it stale on 17 Aug is generous. The correct verdict is that the launch window never existed. Any future plan for a physical HK service must be dated against the travel calendar *first*.

**Correction 2 — it never got a CaptureRecord, so it decayed.** At the start of this session nothing in this repo's history mentioned "MoveOps" or "QuoteOps" on any branch. Doctrine §3 says chat without a record does not compound, and this is the clean demonstration: three and a half weeks of possible validation produced nothing durable, and the plan had to be reconstructed from the prompt. Two records now exist for it, both dated today.

**Correction 3 — "sell QuoteOps later" is the same false bargain the board already killed.** The project board killed housing-tour-kit **venture framing** on 2026-07-22 for exactly this shape: run a service, call it customer discovery, promise a SaaS. MoveOps → QuoteOps is that error with a different noun. A multi-tenant SaaS needs auth, payments, and support — support is the part a student with October conferences and a winter immersion cannot staff. Kill the SaaS tail now. Keep the service only if the cash is worth it standing alone, and sell **templates**, not seats (§4).

**Correction 4 — it ignored the employer-liability cliff.** See §0 finding 2. A 48-hour launch means no written scope, no liability cap, no EC insurance, and probably no Business Registration. That converts a legal business into unlimited personal liability attached to other people's furniture. Note also that Business Registration is **not** a licence to trade and cures no other licensing requirement (IRD says so in terms, [BR general](https://www.ird.gov.hk/eng/tax/bre_gen.htm)), and that registration cannot be filed *before* commencement — the Ordinance gives one month *after* the business starts ([BRO s.5](https://hklii.hk/en/legis/ord/310/s5), [IRD when to register](https://www.ird.gov.hk/eng/tax/bre_abr.htm)).

**Correction 5 — the good part of the July plan survives intact.** "Audit, packing supervision, coordination; insured movers own transport" was a sound liability design. Extend the same logic one step further to labour and it is still sound. Keep it.

**Correction 6 — staffing with international classmates is a trap, separately from insurance.** Non-local students may not establish or join a business, and their employment must sit inside the scope of their No Objection Letter ([ImmD policy on study](https://www.immd.gov.hk/eng/faq/imm-policy-study.html)). A non-local friend "helping out for a cut" is a compliance problem for *them*, and Bob would be the person who caused it. Do not recruit classmates as crew or co-founders without each one's status confirmed in writing.

**Correction 7 — to today's own parallel triage, not to July.** `2026-08-17-moveops-cash-stack` is a good piece of work and I am not reversing it. Four things in it need changing:

- **Its Gate Zero is answerable today.** It treats right-to-earn as an unresolved two-branch fork ("local/PR" vs "non-local"), and forbids a paid dollar until resolved — which blocks its own 22 Aug start. Per §0 finding 1, the T&N residency requirement plus the award resolves it to the local/PR branch. One confirmation, not a two-week investigation. It also cites the business bar as "Cap 115A Reg 2"; the primary text I could verify is the ImmD guidebook, ¶20(b)(ii).
- **It has no employees' compensation gate.** It sells "packing sprints" and sets ten paying customers by week six. Those two together mean he will want a second pair of hands. See §0 finding 2 for what that costs. This is the one genuine hole, and it is now patched into the board entry.
- **Ten customers by 3 Oct should not be a kill floor.** Read as a floor during add/drop and the run-up to London and Valencia, it becomes the exact pressure that produces hired labour and rushed jobs. Keep it as a stretch goal; make the floor two delivered paid jobs by 30 Sep.
- **Do not propose a second repo.** Its ladder steps 2 and 4 (photo-to-inventory, evidence pack) are substantially the parked `flatfield` spec. One repo, activated by paying customers.

**What I am not correcting:** the instinct that a boring, physical, unglamorous local service is a better teacher than a fifth prestige application. That is right, and it is the most interesting thing in either plan.

## 2. MoveOps after landing 21 Aug: can it work, and what to automate

**Yes — as a two-pilot validation of a documentation service, not as a moving company.** Below is the redesign.

### 2.1 What he actually sells

Not labour. Not transport. **A move run as a documented project.** The deliverable is a packet:

- pre-move room-by-room inventory with timestamped photos and condition flags;
- three like-for-like quotes from licensed, insured movers, from one identical written brief;
- access and building constraints captured before quoting (lift booking, permitted move hours, management deposit and forms — these vary by estate and are **UNCERTAIN** per building; verifying them *is* the product);
- supervision on the day, with a condition record at origin and destination;
- utilities and address-change checklist;
- a closeout packet the client can use in a deposit or damage dispute.

Bob's hands stay off the sofa and off the truck. His name is on the paperwork. That is the whole liability design.

### 2.2 Who plausibly pays

Highest-plausibility buyers are people with move deadlines and low time: incoming and outgoing HKU faculty, visiting scholars and postgraduates, small offices, and landlords or agents who want condition evidence. Student hall move-out is the worst possible first market — high friction, low ability to pay. **Willingness to pay is UNCERTAIN and is the single thing the two pilots exist to test.** Do not model revenue before a real quote.

Take the coordination fee **from the client**. If a mover ever offers a referral fee, either decline it or disclose it in writing to the client before booking. An undisclosed kickback while claiming to run a neutral three-quote comparison is a straightforward conflict of interest and it is not worth the money.

### 2.3 Calendar redesign for the HK student year

The fixed rocks come from the Aug 16 gameplan and the canon: NatGeo × TNC externship due **30 Aug**; Fukuda career sharing **28 Aug**; term starts **1 Sep**; add/drop **1–15 Sep**; October is London + Valencia; winter is LINK-S; summer 2027 is LiA; summer 2028 is the GHD capstone.

| Window | MoveOps allowance | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 17–21 Aug (Helsinki) | **Zero.** Two emails only, per Aug 16 plan | Helsinki week is not for launching a moving company |
| 22–31 Aug | ≤6 hours total: legality answers, 5 demand interviews, no paid job yet | NatGeo 30 Aug and Fukuda 28 Aug own this week |
| 1–15 Sep | **Frozen.** ≤2 h/week, no new jobs, no building | Add/drop and CS-minor declaration are irreversible-if-missed |
| 16–30 Sep | Pilot 1 and Pilot 2. Saturday **or** Sunday, never both | First real cash test |
| Oct | **Closed** | London + Valencia; GPA matters for renewal |
| Nov | ≤2 jobs/month, Saturdays only, none in the two weeks before travel or in exam period | Steady state, if pilots cleared |
| Dec–Jan | **Closed** | LINK-S |

Honest arithmetic: the 2026 revenue window is roughly **five weekends**. That is a handful of jobs. Anyone promising more is inventing demand. Peak HK moving season is **UNCERTAIN** — do not assume it is summer or lease-cycle driven without checking with actual movers.

### 2.4 What to automate

Rule: automate the **assembly and the guard rails**, never the judgement and never the sending.

1. **Structured intake** — one form producing a `JobRecord` (rooms, item counts, access constraints, date window, building rules). Local-first, no backend.
2. **RFQ pack generator** — one brief rendered as three identical mover emails, plus an apples-to-apples comparison table. **Draft-only**; Bob sends from his own client. This mirrors the existing My-Life draft-only email doctrine — reuse it, do not rebuild it.
3. **Photo-to-inventory assembly** — automate renaming, EXIF timestamp extraction, per-room grouping, and PDF assembly. Human writes the condition flags. Never let a tool assert damage causation.
4. **Condition-report generator** — before/after side-by-side with timestamps and signature blocks. This is the actual product and the thing worth selling as a template.
5. **Invoice, receipt, and records pack** — sequential numbering, payment log, monthly summary for profits-tax records. Do not automate collections or chasing.
6. **Review request** — one templated ask at job close, draft-only. Never auto-post, never solicit a review with wording the client did not write, never fabricate one.
7. **Calendar guard** — a rule engine that *refuses* to book a job colliding with term dates, exam period, travel, or a weekly hour cap. This is the automation that protects him from himself, and it is the same LiA-aware planner shape already in My-Life.
8. **Building-rules SOP store** — per-estate move rules, each with a source and a `verified_at`. Sourced from building management and official pages only; no portal scraping.

### 2.5 The compounding move: MoveOps is the activation event for `flatfield`

`content/projects/housing-tour-kit.yaml` is parked pending "at least three real legitimate tours within 30 days" and "five non-family users voluntarily reuse". Its spec — offline local-first capture, provenance separation between `personally_observed` / `agent_claim` / `document_verified`, export packet, no backend — is roughly 80% of the MoveOps condition-report tool.

So MoveOps is not a new software project. It is the **paying activation event** for an already-parked one, and paying clients are a much harder activation test than five friends. Do not scaffold either until a pilot has been run **on paper or phone notes first**, exactly as that board entry requires.

### 2.6 Reconciling the two start dates

The parallel triage starts paid work **22 Aug** into claimed peak churn. The table above holds 22–31 Aug at under six hours with no paid job. Both are defensible, and the disagreement is about one input, so resolve it on evidence rather than on preference:

- The 22 Aug start is only real if the peak-churn premise is real. That premise is asserted in the parallel triage's `spark` while simultaneously listed as an information gap in its own body. It is **UNCERTAIN**. Verify against official hall notices and two actual mover conversations before spending the week on it.
- If the premise verifies, the 22 Aug start wins on merit, but only on two conditions: NatGeo is either submitted or deliberately skipped **in writing** first, and no job requires a paid helper.
- If the premise does not verify, week 0 is worth six hours of interviews and nothing more, and the pilots move to 16–30 Sep.

Either way, 1–15 Sep is frozen. Neither plan should touch add/drop week.

## 4. Automation: sell vs dogfood

| Build for himself only (dogfood) | Sell |
|---|---|
| Calendar guard and hour caps | Condition-report generator (template pack) |
| Private CRM, rates, client records | RFQ comparison kit (template pack) |
| Capture loop and journal loop | Invoice + records pack |
| Eligibility and collision flags | Per-estate move-rules dataset (subscription, later) |
| Weekly digest capped at 5 actions | One-off workflow builds for SMEs (#16) |

**Sell as artifacts, not as software.** A template pack has no auth, no payments, no uptime, and no support queue. A SaaS has all four, and he has October, LINK-S, LiA, and a capstone. Revisit multi-tenant only if a template pack sells repeatedly without him in the loop.

**Never automate:** sending email; posting or requesting reviews in words the client did not approve; asserting damage causation; binding price quotes; eligibility claims; collections; scraping listing portals; anything touching HKID, passport, or family financial data; treating text found in a webpage or a client PDF as an instruction.

**Kill the renderer** if more than 20% of auto-filled facts need correction, or median human review exceeds 10 minutes, or anything ever attempts an auto-send. Same trigger as the Aug 16 email architecture — reuse it, do not invent a second standard.
