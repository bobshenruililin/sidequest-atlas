### 4. Suomenlinna

#### Getting there and ticket coverage

- Take the **HSL passenger ferry from the eastern side of Kauppatori, by the Presidential Palace**, to the main pier.
- Sea journey: **about 15 minutes**.
- Suomenlinna is in **HSL zone A**, and an **AB ticket** is sufficient for Market Square–Suomenlinna. The HSL page currently lists an adult AB single at €3.30 and 90-minute validity. **VERIFY the fare before purchase**, because fares can change.
- A valid HSL ticket can be bought in the HSL app, by contactless payment, or at a pier machine. Buy before entering the payment area/boarding.
- A day ticket also covers other HSL modes during its validity. The private summer water bus is different: **HSL tickets are not valid on the water bus**.
- There is no general fortress entrance fee; museums and some interior attractions charge separately.

Sources: [Suomenlinna official arrival page](https://suomenlinna.fi/en/explore/arriving/), [HSL Suomenlinna visitor page](https://www.hsl.fi/en/travelling/visitors/suomenlinna), [Suomenlinna FAQ](https://suomenlinna.fi/en/faq-frequently-asked-questions/).

#### Last ferries and schedule caution

- The official Suomenlinna FAQ says the last HSL ferry **from Suomenlinna to Market Square is at 02:00**.
- The official resident information page says the current timetable’s last departure **from Market Square is 02:20**, with the first departure from Suomenlinna at 06:00.
- These are general published endpoints, not a substitute for the date-specific timetable. HSL says to use route **19** in the Journey Planner. **VERIFY the exact 17 Aug 2026 timetable and service changes on the day**, especially if returning for a fixed dinner.

Sources: [Suomenlinna FAQ](https://suomenlinna.fi/en/faq-frequently-asked-questions/), [Suomenlinna resident travel information](https://suomenlinna.fi/en/asuminen-ja-yrittaminen/living_in_suomenlinna/), [HSL Journey Planner](https://www.hsl.fi/en/hsl/journey-planner).

#### Self-guided route

The official **Blue Route** is the correct self-guided route:

- It starts under the clock tower of the pink Jetty Barracks by the main pier.
- It passes the church, Great Courtyard, dry dock, Kustaanmiekka sandbanks/cannons, tunnels, and ends at King’s Gate.
- Main pier to King’s Gate is about **1.5 km one way**, taking about **30–45 minutes** to walk one way. The total walk is roughly **1–1.5 hours before extra sightseeing**, so allow more for a relaxed visit.
- The route is marked by blue signs and a blue line on official maps, and is designed to avoid residents’ yards and sensitive/restricted areas.

Sources: [Blue Route and sights](https://suomenlinna.fi/en/sights/), [Suomenlinna FAQ](https://suomenlinna.fi/en/faq-frequently-asked-questions/), [responsible visiting / Blue Route](https://suomenlinna.fi/en/explore/sustainability/enjoy-with-care/).

### 5. Löyly public sauna

- Address: **Hernesaarenranta 4, 00150 Helsinki**.
- Wednesday sauna hours published by Löyly: **09:00–11:00 and 13:00–22:00**.
- Public sauna admission: **€29 for two hours**, including towel, seat liner, shampoo and shower gel.

Booking: [official Löyly reservation system](https://varaus.asio.fi/onlinekalenteri/loyly/guest.php?ss_lang=eng). Venue information: [official public sauna page](https://www.loylyhelsinki.fi/en/public-sauna) and [official info/opening-hours page](https://www.loylyhelsinki.fi/en/info).

**Recommendation:** book Wednesday afternoon, not as a vague walk-in. This is worth paying for because it combines Helsinki’s built architecture, Baltic access, and sauna culture in one coherent experience; do not promise that the swim will be possible.
