# Helsinki neighbourhood eateries, 16–20 August 2026

This is the deeper neighbourhood list, not another Nolla/Grön/Fazer recap. It prioritises Punavuori, the Design District, Ullanlinna, Kamppi, Kallio, Töölö, Telakka/Hietalahti/Hernesaari, Hakaniemi Market Hall and Vanha Kauppahalli.

## Read this first

- The window is **Sunday 16, Monday 17, Tuesday 18, Wednesday 19 and Thursday 20 August 2026**.
- Every time below is **VERIFY**, even when copied from an official page on 15 August. Check the linked page again before setting out; summer hours and private events move.
- “Open in the window” means the published weekly pattern includes that day. It is not a live reservation or a guarantee that the kitchen has space.
- No unsupported price, opening-hour or event claim is included. If an allowed source did not publish current hours, this says so.
- **GO** = worth choosing as a meal. **OPTION** = useful in the right neighbourhood. **SKIP-AS-DESTINATION** = fine only for convenience. **VERIFY-ONLY** = existence is supported, but current operating details are not.
- Booking advice is a planning recommendation. “Walk-in” does not promise immediate seating.

## The shortest useful answer

If Bob only has room for a few non-obvious meals:

1. **Katana** for Helsinki’s new-wave ramen, but verify hours directly.
2. **Sushi Wagocoro** for a tiny Japanese-owned neighbourhood sushi room; book/call first.
3. **Georgian Kitchen** for the most personal central Georgian table; **Georgian Supra** if the evening belongs in Kallio.
4. **Mountain or Sansar** for the ordinary Helsinki Nepalese lunch that visitors routinely overlook.
5. Pick **one** classic: **Sea Horse** for the strongest time capsule, **Kosmos** for “Helsinki cuisine,” or **Cella** for the non-grand Kallio version.
6. **BasBas**, **Nolita** or **The Alley** for small plates; **Harju 8** when reservations would kill the mood.
7. **Way Kallio**, **Café Succès**, **Hopia** or **Eromanga** for a bakery stop with more local specificity than Fazer.
8. At the halls, choose **Lohtu or SOUP+MORE in Hakaniemi** for ordinary lunch; use **Story or SOUP+MORE in Vanha Kauppahalli** when the harbour location matters.

## Editorial skips even when open

- Sushi buffet chains: they do not answer the requested sushi brief.
- “Finnish experience” rooms whose main product is costume/postcard atmosphere: Sea Horse, Kosmos, Elite, Cella and Kannas provide stronger living continuity.
- A multi-stall market-hall crawl: choose one counter and spend the saved appetite on a neighbourhood dinner.
- Crossing the city for a merely convenient option: Ichiraku, Salve, Manala and Mount Kailash are useful when already nearby, not mandatory detours.
