## 3. Other companies and institutions (higher ROI than Nokia reception)

**Monday 17 Aug (already the Aalto day)**

[Independent exploration](https://www.aalto.fi/en/visiting-aalto-university/independent-exploration). Design Factory (Viima) student-access holiday ended **17 Aug** — public first-floor areas exist during building hours; do not assume labs. *Designs for a Cooler Planet* opens **1 Sep** — after you leave.
