# HKU “Future Leader(s) Scholarship” — you cannot apply

**Verdict:** **No.** There is no HKU scheme by that name that a Year-2 BASc(GHD) student can apply to. Two real instruments share the branding. Both miss you. Do not write to either office asking for an exception.

Crawled 23 Aug 2026. Do not invent a 2026/27 FBE deadline; the public programme page still shows the **2025–26** round.

## The false bargain: “GHD collaborates with the Business School”

GHD’s own faculty page says the programme is hosted by SPH and offered **in collaboration with** Social Sciences and HKU Business School. Admissions copy lists Business and Economics among faculties that teach into the interdisciplinary major.

Collaboration ≠ enrolment. Taking FBE courses, a CS minor, or a business elective does not make you “a student studying in the HKU Business School.” Do not email FBE UG enrichment to argue the seam. That is prestige-hunger lawyering.

GPA 4.09 / 4.30 would clear the FBE CGPA 3.0 / top-15% bar **if** you were FBE. You are not. The bar is irrelevant.

## If a 2026/27 FBE round opens

Still **no**, unless you have transferred into an FBE undergraduate curriculum — which you will not, because GHD stay is canon.

Do not watch the Qualtrics link. Do not draft the 500-word career essay. HK$20,000 one-off is real money for an FBE Year 2; it is not a leadership identity, and it is not on-thesis.
