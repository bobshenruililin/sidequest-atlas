## 0. How to read this

You already pay **HKU local tuition**, and Tung & Ngai covers it. That is the whole arbitrage. A year at Chicago billed as a Chicago student is in the high five figures of US dollars. A year at Chicago billed as an HKU exchange student is HKU composition fee — which T&N pays. The “steal” is real. It is also **not unique to Chicago**, not free of living costs, and not the same thing as a public-health degree.

What you asked for (Chicago, Columbia, LSE, Todai, Waseda, Kyoto, AFPLA at PKU/Fudan) is treated as a **named set**, not as the only schools that exist. Peer cards exist so prestige-myopia does not eat the preference list.

**Subagents used (2026-08-19):** Sol (GPT-5.6) on value/calendar; Fable (Claude Fable 5) on experience/access; Opus 5 on an adversarial GHD-tool review (companion: `docs/exchange-review-ghd-hkuww.md`); regional crawls for Japan, Mainland, and Anglosphere. This document is the synthesis, not a paste of those chats. Where Opus treated the **March leftover PDF** as the full catalogue, that error is discarded here and flagged in the companion’s editor’s note.

**How numbers are labelled**

- **Published / dated:** copied from an official page with an access date.
- **Arithmetic:** FX conversion of a published figure. Rounded to the nearest HK$1,000 unless the source is already round.

- **Proxy:** 2026/27 quota used as *shape* for 2027/28. The 27/28 partner PDF is not out.

Never treat a leftover-round inventory as a cancellation.

## 2. The physics of one slot

IAO eligibility (2027/28 page, accessed 2026-08-19):

- Non-final year; cannot apply if you will be in the second-to-last or last semester.
- **Year 2+ priority** over Year 1. You will be Y2 in Oct 2026. This is the intended window.
- **Not joined any previous semester exchange or special visiting programme organised by IAO, faculties or departments.**
- CGPA 3.0+ “relatively higher chance.” Assessment uses **CGPA as of Semester 1 of the application year**.
- IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 93 for IAO. Hosts are often higher (Chicago IELTS 7.0 all subs / TOEFL 104; Columbia IELTS 7.5 / TOEFL 105). IB English A 7 may satisfy *some* IAO uploads; hosts still often want a test. **Book IELTS/TOEFL so the report is valid through host deadlines (often Apr 2027 for FY).**
- Personal statement: **100 words**. Full-year applicants must justify full year because FY quota is scarce.
- Deposit HK$2,000 on acceptance (12 Feb 2027 for main round).

**What does *not* burn the slot (current published taxonomy — Horizons splits “Exchange/Visiting” from “Short-term Study/Study Trip”):**

- China Vision short programmes (Mainland Affairs), typically 2–5 weeks.
- LINK (Teaching & Learning): 12-credit HKU course with a **3-week** Shanghai or HCMC immersion.
- GHD **capstone field placement** (separate SPH mechanism).
- Wu Zhi Qiao / service (already done).
- Recognized Programmes summer sessions (fee-paying; PKU SSI, Fudan ISS, UTokyo GUC, etc.).

**What *does* burn the slot:** HKUWW semester or year; faculty/department semester exchange; Worldwide Plus **visiting** (explicitly “special visiting programmes organised by IAO”).

**Uncertainty you must close in writing:** whether Tung & Ngai’s “compulsory Mainland exchange” can be satisfied by LINK, China Vision summer, T&N’s own 10-day Dandelion programme, or only by a semester. Foundation text says **flexibility on programme duration** and the 2025 annual report *promotes* Tsinghua camp and a 10-day Beijing service-learning option. That is not a written waiver. Ask AASO / Foundation liaison before you bet a year of Chicago on it.

A second uncertainty: the eligibility clause is broad (“special visiting programmes organised by IAO, faculties or departments”). LINK is Teaching & Learning, not IAO, and Horizons classes it as short-term. **Get IAO (`goabroad@hku.hk`) to confirm in writing before you enrol.** Being wrong costs the slot.

### What IAO actually scores

Published: CGPA (S1 of application year), 100-word PS, English, interview if any. The 100 words must cover why exchange, why first-choice institution, why first-choice country, and **if FY, why a year**. FY quota is the scarce column; a FY PS that could equally describe a semester is a wasted shot.

### Credit transfer physics (Senate policy, not a vibe)

- A host **semester** of full-time study normally converts to **up to 30 HKU credits, maximum 36**.
- A host **year** normally converts to **up to 60, maximum 72**.
- Hard ceiling **120 transferred credits** across the degree.
- Approval **before departure**. Whole courses only for major mapping; rejects fall through to electives.
- Host grades **do not enter GPA** and typically **do not appear** on the HKU transcript.

Faculty of Social Sciences publishes a “50% of major at HKU, including capstone” rule. **That is FSS, not Medicine.** The HKUMed/SPH equivalent for BASc(GHD) is **UNVERIFIED**. Obtain it in writing. GHD Year 3 is 66 credits with **48 elective** — structurally the year built to absorb a *semester*. A full year fights `GHAD3001`.

### Faculty column

GHD is hosted by **SPH / LKS Medicine**. HKUWW faculty earmarks for you are likely **Medicine**. Many hosts mark Medicine N/A; that usually means **MBBS/clinical**, not “no GHD student may apply.” PKU’s line is harsher because it names programmes **or courses**. UBC Oct 2025: 30 spots with Medicine earmark **3** and no medicine-course block on the PDF line. McGill: Medicine programmes unavailable, yet Medicine seats exist. **Inference, not fact:** Medicine-earmarked seats may be a thinner queue because MBBS cohorts cannot easily leave. Verify with IAO.
