## 0. How to read this

You already pay **HKU local tuition**, and Tung & Ngai covers it. That is the whole arbitrage. A year at Chicago billed as a Chicago student is in the high five figures of US dollars. A year at Chicago billed as an HKU exchange student is HKU composition fee — which T&N pays. The “steal” is real. It is also **not unique to Chicago**, not free of living costs, and not the same thing as a public-health degree.

What you asked for (Chicago, Columbia, LSE, Todai, Waseda, Kyoto, AFPLA at PKU/Fudan) is treated as a **named set**, not as the only schools that exist. Peer cards exist so prestige-myopia does not eat the preference list.

**Fable** (Claude Fable 5) and **Sol** (GPT-5.6 Sol) were spawned for experience/access and for value/calendar. Regional crawls covered Japan, Mainland, and the Anglosphere. This document is the synthesis, not a paste of three chats.

4. **AFPL in your usage is AFPLA** — Asian Future Political Leaders Association. Five universities: PKU, Fudan, UTokyo, SNU, NTU. Student club + annual summer forum. Not a dual degree, not an exchange programme, **not a guaranteed seat** for an HKU inbound.
5. **Spending HKUWW on PKU/Fudan and later spending HKUWW on Chicago is not allowed** under current IAO rules: previous IAO/faculty/department semester or visiting exchange disqualifies you. Faculty pages also say at most one offer across HKUWW / faculty / departmental.
6. **GHD is not MBBS, but PKU still blocks the Health Science Center** for standard exchange. “Medicine programmes or courses are not available” on the Oct 2025 list. PKU ISD/HSC fact sheets: Health Science Center not available to ordinary exchange students. A Beida semester is a **university** semester, not a SPH semester, unless you find a faculty-specific channel.
7. **The March 2026 PDF is leftovers.** Chicago and Columbia vanish from it. UCEAP pool drops from 70 (Oct) to 18 S1 (March). Waseda and Kyoto also drop off the leftover sheet. An adversarial pass that treated the March PDF as the full catalogue concluded Chicago/Columbia/Waseda were *unavailable* — that is a **list-artefact error**, not a programme cancellation. The 9 Oct 2025 sheet is the last *complete* published 2026/27 catalogue: Chicago 4 FY, Columbia 6 S, Waseda 2 FY, Kyoto 1 FY. The 27/28 list is not published yet. Plan from Oct 2025 *shape*, never from leftover inventory.
8. **Exchange grades do not enter your HKU GPA.** BASc(GHD) regulations and the Senate credit-transfer policy: transferred credits appear, **host results are not in GPA**, and host grades typically do not appear on the HKU transcript. T&N renewal at 3.50 is therefore **not** threatened by a brutal Core. The real cost of a full year is the **opposite**: you convert ~60 credits from *graded HKU evidence* into ungraded transfer. For a 4.09 student, a year spends the asset rather than growing it.
9. **AFPL is two different objects.** **AFPLA** = student forum (PKU, Fudan, Todai, SNU, NTU). **AFLSP** = Bai Xian Asia Institute scholarship (partners include PKU, Tsinghua, SJTU, Kyoto, Waseda, CUHK, HKUST — **not HKU**). No HKUWW destination makes you an AFLSP scholar. Postgraduate at a BXAI partner is the real door.

## 2. The physics of one slot

IAO eligibility (2027/28 page, accessed 2026-08-19):

- Non-final year; cannot apply if you will be in the second-to-last or last semester.
- **Year 2+ priority** over Year 1. You will be Y2 in Oct 2026. This is the intended window.
- **Not joined any previous semester exchange or special visiting programme organised by IAO, faculties or departments.**
- CGPA 3.0+ “relatively higher chance.” Assessment uses **CGPA as of Semester 1 of the application year**.
- IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 93 for IAO. Hosts are often higher (Chicago IELTS 7.0 all subs / TOEFL 104; Columbia IELTS 7.5 / TOEFL 105). IB English A 7 may satisfy *some* IAO uploads; hosts still often want a test. **Book IELTS/TOEFL so the report is valid through host deadlines (often Apr 2027 for FY).**
- Personal statement: **100 words**. Full-year applicants must justify full year because FY quota is scarce.
- Deposit HK$2,000 on acceptance (12 Feb 2027 for main round).

**What does *not* burn the slot (current published taxonomy):**

- China Vision short programmes (Mainland Affairs / Horizons “short-term study”), typically 2–5 weeks.
- LINK (Teaching & Learning): 12-credit HKU course with a **3-week** Shanghai or HCMC immersion.
- GHD **capstone field placement** (separate SPH mechanism).
- Wu Zhi Qiao / service (already done).

**What *does* burn the slot:** HKUWW semester or year; faculty/department semester exchange; Worldwide Plus **visiting** (explicitly “special visiting programmes organised by IAO”).

**Uncertainty you must close in writing:** whether Tung & Ngai’s “compulsory Mainland exchange” can be satisfied by LINK, China Vision summer, T&N’s own 10-day Dandelion programme, or only by a semester. Foundation text says **flexibility on programme duration** and the 2025 annual report *promotes* Tsinghua camp and a 10-day Beijing service-learning option. That is not a written waiver. Ask AASO / Foundation liaison before you bet a year of Chicago on it.

## 12. Rigor, letters, and what GHD actually needs

GHD is hosted by **SPH / LKS Medicine**, taught with Social Sciences, Business, Law, Science, Architecture. HKUWW faculty column for you is likely **Medicine**. Many hosts mark Medicine N/A. That usually means **MBBS/clinical**, not “no GHD student may apply.” PKU’s line is harsher because it names programmes **or courses**.

What actually compounds for *your* thesis (CHP, China CDC, HK as seam, heat-health Laidlaw):

- Methods that travel: epi, health economics, policy evaluation, institutions.
- Letters from people who saw you do hard work (host transcript may never show on HKU GPA — letters matter more).
- Mandarin professional register.
- **Not missing GHAD3001 / the capstone.**

Chicago Core + social science can mint the letter. It can also be the wrong building and the wrong calendar. LSE is development/econometrics, not Health Policy department. PKU/Fudan is T&N-native and may not include SPH (PKU HSC blocked; Fudan SPH **unverified**). **UBC is the path your programme has already walked**, with named undergrad population-health courses — boring, therefore informative.

**Do not use exchange to skip the capstone.** The capstone is the job-like semester. Exchange is school. A full-year Y3 that forces the capstone to slide is the largest unforced error on the board — unless SPH writes that GHAD3001 can move.
