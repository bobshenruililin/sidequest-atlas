# Interview Craft — Distilled Brief (Sol)

*Synthesized 2026-07-27 for T&N panel 28 Jul.*

## What panels select for (beyond CV)

- **Coherence** — choices form a believable arc  
- **Presence** — listen, answer the asked question, stay composed  
- **Teachability** — revise without defensiveness  
- **Mission fit** — Foundation as platform, not trophy  
- **Judgment** — impressive ≠ important  
- **Contribution** — what you bring to a cohort  

Silent question: *Would we trust this person with opportunity, ambiguity, and other people?*

## Stand out in 15 minutes without slickness

Answer shape: **headline → one concrete example → what changed → (optional) mission link.**  
45–75 seconds. Stop when done.

Rhodes/Marshall transferable lesson: overly rehearsed = “superficially slick.” Memorize *ideas*, not paragraphs. Keep one imperfect truthful detail. Admit trade-offs. Let follow-ups happen.

## Psychology

Anxiety eats working memory. Goal ≠ zero anxiety; goal = stop fighting it.

**CBT swaps**

| Hot thought | Cooler thought |
|-------------|----------------|
| I must impress | Help them understand me accurately |
| Pause = weakness | Pause = selection and control |
| Perfect answer | One honest claim + one useful example |
| They are judging me | We are testing mutual fit |
| One weak answer ruins all | Panels assess the whole interaction |
| Anxiety = failing | Activation means this matters |

**Outcome independence:** You control listening, honesty, length, pace, recovery, language requests. You do not control chemistry or seats.

**When WM drops:** exhale → feet down → repeat core noun in question → headline first → one example.

## Emotion / energy

Warmth = greeting, listening face, slower by ~10%, eye contact across panel.  
Vulnerability = difficulty → action/learning → changed practice (not raw pain for effect).  
Day-of: sleep, familiar food, normal caffeine, **minimize Canto drilling in last hours**, enter at **80% energy**.

## Cantonese L2 (Mandarin/English thinker)

Canto costs WM because you do not *think* in it. Heavy self-monitoring (tones + grammar + content + impression) = cascade failure.

- English primary; Canto for brief relational or already-stable answers  
- Chunks not essays; SVO; accept imperfect tones if meaning clear  
- Switch politely when nuance decays — precision > belonging theatre  

## Target

Not flawless multilingual theatre. A coherent, teachable, mission-aligned person who stays present when the conversation becomes real.
