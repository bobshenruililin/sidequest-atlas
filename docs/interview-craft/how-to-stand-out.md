# How to stand out (15 minutes)

## The paradox

Standing out ≠ being the most impressive CV. Shortlistees are already impressive. Standing out = being the person whose **thinking is visible and trustworthy** in the room.

## Differentiation levers (ranked for you)

| Lever | Why it works for T&N | How |
|-------|----------------------|-----|
| **Specific Mainland service** | Rare vs finance/tour gravity | One Gansu image + what collaboration changed |
| **Honest tenses** | Many inflate future | Done / ongoing / upcoming / aim — say it |
| **Research→service without hiding research** | HKU stressed service; T&N scholars often research-forward | Prerequisite frame, not apology |
| **Teachability** | Soft rubric 跟隨→創造 | Admit a revised belief |
| **Cohort contribution** | 2025 survey: community + mentorship hunger | “Peers more capable because…” |
| **Refuse temptations** | Kissinger diagnostic | Name one prestige you’d reject |

## Answer architecture

```text
1. Direct answer (1 sentence)
2. One concrete scene (20–30s)
3. What changed / trade-off (1 sentence)
4. Optional: link to Foundation / HK return (1 sentence)
STOP
```

Time box: **45–75s**. If they want more, they ask.

## Anti-slick checklist

- [ ] No triple-list of achievements unless asked  
- [ ] No jargon stack (interoperability, synergy, bridge) without a noun people can see  
- [ ] No “as one of the youngest…”  
- [ ] No past-tense for London/GEST/LiA/CDC  
- [ ] One human detail that isn’t flattering (disagreement, wrong assumption, unfinished Seam Ready)

## Rhodes / Marshall transferable notes

- Interview often checks whether you *are* the documents.  
- Short answers OK; interruption may test agility.  
- “I don’t know” + what you’d do to find out > bluff.  
- Conversation > interrogation.  
- Over-prep that kills personality is a known failure mode.

## Soft open discipline (email frame)

Background → achievements → aspirations. Then **stop**. Let them steer. Do not continue into a second speech.

## Questions you may ask (if offered)

Pick one; must not be Googleable:

- How do inaugural scholars typically use the Endeavour Fund well?  
- What does the Foundation hope the scholar community will look like in five years?  
- For public-health / medical-services oriented scholars, what Mainland immersion patterns have been most formative?

## What will *not* make you stand out

- Speaking more languages than needed  
- Longer answers  
- Quoting Kissinger / Weber  
- Listing four continents  
- Performing Cantonese belonging
