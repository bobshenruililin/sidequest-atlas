# Psychology and emotion

## What interview anxiety actually does

From CBT and L2 anxiety research (Horwitz tradition; Attentional Control / Processing Efficiency accounts):

1. Threat thoughts (“I’ll freeze”) trigger arousal.  
2. Arousal steals **working memory** needed for planning speech.  
3. In L2 (esp. Cantonese for you), conceptualization + formulation + monitoring compete harder.  
4. You fill pauses with “um,” speed up, or go blank — which confirms the threat thought.

So the technical fix is: **lower monitoring load** and **pre-load chunks**, not “be confident.”

## CBT micro-protocol (2 minutes)

When a hot thought appears, ask:

1. Fact or prediction?  
2. Evidence for / against?  
3. Absolute language (“always/never”)?  
4. What would I tell a friend?  
5. Useful action now?

Write one disputed thought tonight:

| Hot thought | Evidence against | Replacement | If it happens |
|-------------|------------------|-------------|----------------|
| I’ll blank in Canto | I have rescue chunks + switch protocol | Pause → scaffold → or switch | 「可唔可以俾我諗一諗先」 |

## Presence psychology (what “stands out” feels like to them)

Scholarship panels (synthesis of fellowship guides + Rhodes/Marshall notes):

- They already believe you are capable enough to be shortlisted.  
- They test **coherence** (does the person match the paper?), **presence**, **teachability**, **mission fit**.  
- Memorable ≠ loud. Memorable = *specific, listening, able to revise*.  
- Over-prep that produces slickness backfires (“too rehearsed,” “superficially slick”).

**Outcome independence:** treat the room as mutual fit-testing. Desperation reads as neediness; calm reads as surplus capacity.

## Emotion regulation day-of

| State | Do | Don’t |
|-------|-----|--------|
| High arousal | Longer exhale than inhale ×5; feet flat; cold water on wrists | Fight the feeling; more caffeine |
| Flat / numb | Short walk; upright posture; speak one sentence aloud | Scroll phone doom |
| Shame after weak answer | “Next question owns the floor” — panels average | Replay and collapse |
| Pride / rush | Soften volume 10%; invite a question back if natural | Dominate |

## Warmth without performance

- Soft smile at greeting (not interview grin).  
- Nod sparingly — continuous nodding looks anxious.  
- Mirror their pace slightly.  
- If interrupted: stop mid-sentence cleanly; do not finish the old thought over them.

## Vulnerability formula (safe)

> Difficulty → what I did → what changed in my practice.

Unsafe: unresolved distress that makes the panel become your therapist; trauma dump; false self-deprecation about competence you actually have.

## Energy budget for 15 minutes

Enter at **~80%**. Peak intensity early → crash mid-panel. Controlled warmth lasts the whole slot.
