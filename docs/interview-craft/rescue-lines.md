# Rescue lines

## Blank / WM drop

**EN**
- Let me take a moment to choose the most relevant example.  
- The short answer is ___. The reason is…  
- There are two parts; the more important is…  
- Could I take a few seconds to think?

**YUE**
- 可唔可以俾我諗一諗先？  
- 我最想講嘅重點係……  

**CMN**
- 请让我用几秒钟想一个最相关的例子。  
- 简单说，……

## Wandering

- Let me give you the direct answer.  
- The central point is…  
- I’ve given too much context — what matters is…  
- 如果用一句講……

## Wrong language / leak

- Sorry — I switched languages. May I continue in English?  
- 唔好意思，我頭先轉咗語言。可唔可以用英文繼續？  
- I’d like to answer in English so I can be more precise — is that alright?

## Interrupted

- Of course — in one sentence: …  
- Understood; the key point is…  
- 明白。我用一句講重點：……

## Didn’t catch the question

- Could you rephrase that?  
- Are you asking about X or Y?  
- 唔好意思，可唔可以再講一次？／講慢少少？

## Challenged / pushed

- That’s a fair concern. My current view is…  
- I may be wrong; the evidence that shaped me was…  
- I would revise that if…  
- The trade-off I see is…

## After a weak answer (internal only)

Next question owns the floor. Do not apologize twice.
