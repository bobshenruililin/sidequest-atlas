# Voice, body, presence

## Before speech

- Feet flat; weight even.  
- Soften jaw; tongue off the roof of the mouth.  
- One slow exhale longer than inhale.  
- Hands visible, resting — not clenched under table.

## While speaking

| Dial | Set to |
|------|--------|
| Speed | 10% slower than normal conversation |
| Volume | Conversational; project slightly if room large |
| Pitch | Avoid rising every sentence (sounds unsure) |
| Eye contact | Questioner → glance others → return |
| Hands | Occasional natural gesture; no choreography |
| Face | Listening face when they speak |

## Multilingual presence

- Language switch: pause, request, continue — looks professional.  
- Apologizing three times for accent looks less professional than one clean switch.  
- Smile at the *start* of a Canto attempt; if it fails mid-way, switch without grimace.

## Interruptions

Stop. Mouth closed. Eyes on interrupter. One-sentence answer. Do not finish the old paragraph.

## Closing presence

Thank them. No extra pitch. Stand when invited. Leave at 80% composure — save the post-mortem for later.
