You enter Year 2 on **1 Sep 2026**, so the degree is not finished this year. SPH copy: field placement **4–6 months at the end of Year 3**. That is a later calendar problem (~2028), not this week. Do not invent a congregation date.

Tung and Ngai’s public eligibility page wants **two years in HK or Mainland after the scholarship**, with flexibility for overseas postgraduate study. A real HK operating-company job would sit on the residence side. An Oxbridge master’s would sit on the flexibility side. Read the **letter**. Do not treat group employment as a way to keep a Foundation scholar title.
