## Route 2 — Foundation PG Oxbridge (live, not this week)

**What it is:** Full-cost postgraduate (Masters or PhD) at Oxford or Cambridge, up to **four years**, tenable only at the eight partner colleges. You need a **provisional university offer**, then the **college** forwards a shortlist to the Foundation. Finalists interview **last week of April to first week of May**; missing that window is a hard fail. Awards include university fees, college fees, stipend, UK Immigration Health Surcharge, and economy airfare. Other scholarships during tenure need trustee consent (regulation (h) in the November 2025 guidelines).

**Official 2026 guidelines (same PDF on Magdalene and Queen’s, issued November 2025):**

- [Magdalene criteria PDF](https://www.magd.cam.ac.uk/sites/default/files/2026-01/Jardine%20Foundation%20General%20Postgraduate%20Criteria%202026.pdf)
- [Queen’s criteria PDF](https://www.queens.ox.ac.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/2026-General-Postgraduate-Criteria.pdf)

**Eligibility (verbatim structure, not a paraphrase of your file):** national **or** resident of Cambodia, mainland China, Hong Kong SAR, Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar, the Philippines, Singapore, Taiwan, Thailand, or Vietnam for **at least seven years**; early career; satisfactory health. No subject restriction in the guidelines. Selection: academic achievement and potential, leadership and fellowship, community involvement, and likelihood of becoming an outstanding citizen with public/community commitment.

**College rules that matter later:**

- Magdalene: one of four Cambridge colleges that can recommend. Complete scholarship applications for **2026 entry** closed **12:00 noon, Monday 23 February 2026**. That clock is **already dead**. Your clock is a future cycle after an Oxbridge offer.
- Queen’s Oxford: **2026 direct-to-college applications closed**. From the **2027** postgraduate admissions cycle, you must **name Queen’s as first-preference college** to use the Queen’s Jardine route. Number available: **varies every year**. ([Queen’s page](https://www.queens.ox.ac.uk/postgraduate-study/jardine-graduate-scholarships/))
- Cambridge Trust lists a partner fund, [Jardine Cambridge Graduate Scholarship](https://www.student-funding.cam.ac.uk/fund/jardine-cambridge-graduate-scholarship-2025), award year **2026/27**, **approximately 5 awards**, tenable at Downing / Magdalene / Peterhouse / Trinity, last updated **29 Jun 2026**. That is a **count**, not a rate, **Cambridge-side only**, and it still requires a separate Foundation application. Do not divide 5 by an invented applicant pool.

**Calendar for you:** Year 2 starts 1 Sep 2026. A GHD degree is not finished this year. A serious Oxbridge postgraduate application is a **final-year / graduation-year** problem (course choice, supervisor, college preference), not a September 2026 form. Do not invent GHD normative length or a 2028 versus 2029 entry year here.
