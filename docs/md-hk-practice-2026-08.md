## Binding filter: MCHK first

All medical graduates who want to register with MCHK, **except graduates of the University of Hong Kong and the Chinese University of Hong Kong**, must pass the Licensing Examination and complete a period of pre-registration internship training and assessment ([MCHK LE general information](https://www.mchk.org.hk/english/exam/general_information.html); page footer last revision December 2017 — use with 2026 LEIP guidance).

**Licensing Examination (current statute, 2026 Guidance Notes).** s.7A: good character, plus either:

1. **Form 1A / s.7A(1)(b)(i):** not less than **5 years full-time medical training of a type approved**, including internship, and an acceptable medical qualification. Guideline 1: not eligible if training is **less than 5 years for any reason, including exemption from part of the curriculum** ([Guideline 1 PDF](https://leip.mchk.org.hk/mchkdocs/guidelines1_eligibility.pdf)).
2. **Form 1B / s.7A(1)(b)(ii):** **Hong Kong permanent resident** holding a **recognized medical qualification for s.14C** (Schedule 1A). This is the cleaner overseas route for a 4-year graduate-entry degree **if that exact programme is on Schedule 1A on the enrolment date**.

Guideline 1’s downloaded PDF still quotes an older s.7A without (b)(ii). Prefer the **2026 Second Sitting Guidance Notes**, which quote the current ordinance including the HKPR alternative ([2026 SS Guidance Notes](https://leip.mchk.org.hk/mchkdocs/Guidance%20Notes%20to%20Candidates-English%202026SS.pdf)).

After passing LE: period of assessment **not exceeding 12 months**. Then:

- s.7A(1)(b)(i) path → may apply for **full registration** under s.14.
- s.7A(1)(b)(ii) path → may apply for **special registration** under s.14C.

Fail any one part **5 consecutive times** → Council may prohibit further attempts.

**Special registration (s.14C)** since 2021: practise in **specified institutions** (Hospital Authority, Department of Health, HKU, CUHK), not a general private-practice ticket on day one. “Specified person” includes (a) recognized qualification **and** registration in a qualifying place (HKPR exempt from the extra Academy of Medicine fellowship limb that applies to non-PR), or (b) HKPR who passed LE under (b)(ii) and finished assessment ([MCHK special registration](https://www.mchk.org.hk/english/registration/special_registration.html)).

**Enrolment-date rule (s.14D):** the qualification is recognized for you if, **on the date you enrol**, that body + qualification sit in Part 1 of Schedule 1A. Do not treat today’s list as a promise for a 2029 enrolment.

**List size:** seventh batch gazetted **18 Jun 2026** — **166** qualifications; **161** doctors on special registration locally as of end May 2026 ([GIA 17 Jun 2026](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202606/17/P2026061700379.htm)). Full list: [MCHK PDF as at 18 Jun 2026](https://www.mchk.org.hk/files/Full_List_(1st_to_7th_batch)_(Jun_2026)_eng.pdf).

**Limited registration** is employment-type restricted. It is not a general “land an MD then clinic” door.

**Information gap:** whether a 4-year GEM **alone** ever satisfies s.7A(1)(b)(i)’s five-year-including-internship rule. Official pages do **not** say that a prior BASc plus a 4-year GEM can be aggregated. Do not assume Form 1A works for 4-year GEM. Plan **Schedule 1A + HKPR Form 1B**, or take HKU/CUHK.

### HKU MBBS (Graduate Entry)

Official page: 4 years; quota **25**; Pre-Clinical then Clinical; optional MRes[Med] adds a fifth year ([HKUMed GE](https://hkumed-ugadmissions.hku.hk/ug_programmes/mbbsge/)).

**2026/27 admissions copy (Bob’s cycle is ~2029 — re-read then):**

## Will you come ahead?

**Already ahead:** household assets versus a typical same-age intern’s personal wealth.  
**Behind, if you do medicine:** two or more years of clinical seniority versus the 2025 local MBBS cohort; overseas adds LE/assessment lag.  
**Not a function of GHD prestige:** Faculty of Medicine on the diploma does not mint an MBBS. Scholarships (Tung and Ngai, Martin, Laidlaw) help the **story**, not a published cutoff.  
**Opportunity cost:** four funded local GE years at UGC fees is the cheap **practice-rights** path. International GEM is a high-six-figure (GBP or AUD) tuition purchase that still dumps you into MCHK. Remaining in GHD → health governance / CHP–China CDC thesis is the path that does **not** require surgery and already matches the floor test.

If the question is “will I be richer than doctors my age at 35,” the honest answer is: **you may already be**, on family assets, and a delayed HA salary does not change that. If the question is “will I be a doctor among doctors my age,” you will be **junior**, and only if you pass admissions, training, and MCHK.
