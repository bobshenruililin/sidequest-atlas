## Email rules

- Subject: parseable nouns + ask. Example shape: `Macha field note + GBA site visit request — Shen Ruililin, HKU GHD`.
- First two lines: who he is, what is attached, the ask. No origin story.
- Middle: one interpretation of the artifact (why it is useful *to them*).
- Close: availability in the relevant window; LINK-S / LiA named if they constrain dates; thanks. No “I look forward to leveraging synergies.”
- Length: roughly 150–220 words unless the application portal demands an essay.
- Language: English default; 書面語 Chinese only when the official channel is Chinese (see LINK-S sample register). Do not flatten one into the other.
- Dual audience: a model should be able to extract `{name, university, programme, artifact, ask, dates}` without adjectives.

## Cover letters / academy apps

Scene → gap → what he already did → what this room is for → return line. Leadership as translation. Do not dump the CV.

NEWDAY-alumni applications to *other* academies: write as someone who has **already lived** residential dialogue, not as someone who hopes to discover it. Do not overclaim “I represent Hong Kong.”
