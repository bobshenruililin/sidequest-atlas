# Play 03 — Macha / rural ageing note → GBA or HK NGO (visit)

**Ask type:** `visit` (default). `unpaid-sprint` only if they have a real short scheme.  
**Window:** winter 2026-27 (HK/GBA) or a day either side of LINK-S if Bob is already in the Mainland — do not invent a second trip.  
**Resume variant:** `mainland-field.md`  
**Collision:** LINK-S (coordinate; do not double-book the same week).

WZQ / Macha is **completed** (May–Jun 2026). Gannan Horizons (Mar 2026) is completed. Write as field notes with humility, not as a consultancy.

## Artifact (1–2 days)

Title shape: *What a village visit is actually for — notes from Macha and Gannan, for a team that already works there.*

1. **Object, then thesis:** a road, a clinic waiting pattern, a livelihood detail Bob has already used in operator-approved writing. No mentee names. No precise private addresses. “Macha Village, Gansu” is enough.
2. **What the coordinator role was:** student coordination, not “I ran rural China.”
3. **What he does not know:** Gannan “crossing made me hungry, not full” is already in the voice corpus — keep that epistemic humility.
4. **Offer:** a site visit or office visit with the note attached. Worst case they keep a four-pager that is specific enough to be useful and modest enough not to embarrass him.
5. **HK–Mainland seam (optional last half page):** interoperability as a *question* (how Hong Kong evidence and Mainland rural delivery fail to talk), not as a CHP job claim. Degree aim toward CHP / China CDC is an aim, not a post.

Do not claim Tung & Ngai on this packet unless `cv/` allows it.
