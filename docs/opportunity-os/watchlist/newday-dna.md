## Mechanism (why it worked for Bob)

NEWDAY is a **residential living-together**, not a conference badge.

| Layer | What actually happened (2026) | What to hunt for elsewhere |
|-------|-------------------------------|----------------------------|
| Place | Nansen Academy, Lillehammer; dorms next to the school | Folk-high-school or academy housing, not a hotel ballroom |
| Time | Nine days, 29 Jul–6 Aug 2026 | Long enough to stop performing |
| People | East Asia (incl. Fudan delegates) × Nordic students | Two regions in one house, not a global “UN youth” mash |
| Form | Lectures, walks, shared meals, games, host-home gatherings | Table + walk + someone’s home, not only panels |
| Translator role | Hong Kong between systems | Rooms where that translation is *used* |
| After | Other Nordic cities as ordinary public life | Leave room after the academy; do not pack a second conference |

- Applying to a three-day Model UN as if it were residential dialogue.
- Writing as if 2027 NEWDAY is open, or inventing 2028 dates.
- Spamming NEWDAY 2026 classmates’ personal emails for favours (private lists are off-limits).
