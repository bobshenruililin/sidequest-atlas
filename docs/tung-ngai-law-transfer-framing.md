## 1. What the transfer letter actually argues

**Primary ask:** internal transfer to **Bachelor of Laws**.  
**Secondary ask:** open to a law-centred interdisciplinary pathway such as **Government and Laws**, because interests sit in public law, governance, political theory, institutional accountability, and social welfare.  
**Central claim:** *In either path, commitment is to the study of law.*

**Logic (compressed):**

1. GHD Year 1 clarified direction — it did **not** fail you.  
2. Scale problems (inequality, ageing, urbanisation, climate, cross-border disease control, trust) need institutions, rules, legitimacy, enforceable obligations.  
3. Evidence / economics / public health show what works / is efficient / is necessary; **law** asks what is authorised, enforceable, fair, and durable beyond one administration.  
4. Global health questions *are* legal questions (emergency powers, regulation, rights, discretion, remedies).  
5. Move from **breadth → discipline**: close reading, argument, research, statutory interpretation, case analysis, normative reasoning.  
6. Antecedents: debate, linguistics, maths/physics/olympiad = precision under constraint.  
7. Moral Controversies (CCHU9009 / Birks) sharpened liberty–welfare / rights–authority tensions.  
8. HK common law + Mainland relationship = meaningful place to study for someone in health/regulation/institutional design.  
9. Contribution: interdisciplinary GHD perspective; public-service orientation (government / regulators / PH institutions / IOs / policy); seriousness about HK as legal–policy hub. PCLL acknowledged; broader ambition public-facing, not Magic Circle theatre.  
10. GPA 4.24 cited as capacity signal.

**Date:** 24 May 2026 — this predates the T&N interview (28 Jul) and award (~5 Aug). Interview prep deliberately **banked** “JD after UG / not Year-1 transfer” for the panel. That was interview strategy under then-live uncertainty — not proof the transfer file vanished.
