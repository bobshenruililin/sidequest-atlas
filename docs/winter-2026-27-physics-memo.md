1. **LINK's 2026/27 dates are published and engineered to fit HKU's inter-semester
   gap.** LINK-S (Shanghai) **27 Dec 2026 – 16 Jan 2027**; LINK-V (Ho Chi Minh City)
   **1–20 June 2027**. **[V]**
2. **The binding constraint is the enrollment window, not the trip.** AY2026-27
   Round 2 (add/drop) runs **15 Aug – 4 Sep 2026** — about a fortnight of runway from
   the access date. **[V]**
3. **LINK-S overlaps both HKUWW interview windows** (late Dec 2026, mid-Jan 2027).
   The 11 Dec deadline and 5 Feb results do not collide; only interviews do. **[V]**
   Whether IAO interviews by video is **[UNVERIFIED]**.
4. **Best reading: LINK does not burn HKUWW eligibility** — but the argument is
   textual, not a ruling, and being wrong costs the slot. **[UNVERIFIED]**.
5. **Shanghai and HCMC draw on different, non-substitutable government pots.**
   Shanghai reaches only the Mainland-only MES; HCMC reaches the general
   Belt-and-Road pot. Winter Shanghai spends the narrow instrument and preserves the
   flexible one. **[V]**

## B) Does LINK — or a Recognized winter programme — burn HKUWW eligibility?

**The clause.** HKUWW criterion 3: *"Not joined any previous semester exchange
programmes or special visiting programmes organised by IAO, faculties or
departments."* **[V]**

Three textual arguments that LINK sits outside it:

1. **Organiser.** The clause enumerates *IAO, faculties or departments*. LINK is
   organised by the **Horizons Office** — a central teaching-and-learning office,
   none of those three. **[V]** on both halves; the conclusion is mine.
2. **Category.** The Horizons taxonomy separates **Exchange/Visiting** from
   **Short-term Study/Study Trip**. LINK appears in neither list, so there is no
   published basis for classing it as Exchange/Visiting. **[V]** (absence of
   evidence, not evidence of absence).
3. **Product shape.** IAO defines a visiting programme as **one semester or one
   academic year at a host institution, paying that institution full academic
   tuition**, attending classes with local students. **[V]** LINK is an HKU course
   taught on the HKU campus with a 3-week field component — not a host placement.

**Recognized winter programmes** sit in the same harbour by the same logic: the list
is published under **"Short-term Study"**, is *"solely intended as a reference"* for
programmes HKU students have previously attended, and IAO states that *"unless
otherwise noted … short-term programmes are **not available on an exchange
basis**"* — you pay the host and apply to it directly. **[V]** Weaker corroboration:
the Faculty of Social Sciences FAQ renders the bar as *"any previous **semester**
exchange programmes organised by HKU"* and treats course-based short-term study
abroad as routine. **[V]** as a faculty page, not IAO law.

**The residual risk.** "Special visiting programmes" is undefined on the HKUWW page,
so every reading is interpretation. One email to `goabroad@hku.hk` converts the bet
into a fact and costs nothing: *"Does enrolling in LINK (12-credit Horizons course,
3-week Shanghai immersion), or registering a Recognized winter programme under the
GSP Support Scheme, affect my eligibility under HKUWW criterion 3?"* **Send before
4 Sep.**

## C) Funding physics

Short-term government subsidies, 2026-27: eligible programme period 1 Sep 2026 –
31 Aug 2027; apply **within 1 month of completion**; needs written approval from the
HKU organising unit, host admission letter, completion proof. **[V]**

| Instrument | Shanghai (LINK-S, 21 days) | HCMC (LINK-V, 20 days) |
|---|---|---|
| **SSE** (means-tested: SFO aid or CSSA) | Asia 3,000 + band 14–28 days 6,000 = **max HK$9,000** × finance level, 50% floor | same arithmetic |
| **SSEBR / Non-means-tested SSEBR** | **Excluded** — Asia row reads "excluding the Chinese Mainland, Taiwan and Macao" | Vietnam is B&R: 900 + 1,800 = **max HK$2,700** non-means-tested |
| **Non-means-tested MES** (Mainland only, ≥3 days, **once** per UG) | 900 + 1,800 = **max HK$2,700**, reimbursement | not applicable |

Rows **[V]** from the IAO short-term subsidy tables and the China Vision MES page.
Vietnam's place on EDB's own B&R list is **[UNVERIFIED]** — HKU points at an external
list I did not open.

Three consequences that change the decision:

- **Do not spend SSE on a 3-week trip.** SSE is **once per undergraduate,
  irrespective of amount**. **[V]** A future full-year North American exchange is
  worth 9,000 + 45,000 = HK$54,000 × finance level. Trading that for a HK$9,000
  ceiling is bad. (Whether you are means-tested at all is **[UNVERIFIED]** — I did
  not touch private financial data.)
- **MES has no alternative use.** Mainland-only. Spending it on Shanghai leaves the
  flexible non-means-tested SSEBR (once, up to HK$18,000 lifetime) intact for a
  non-Mainland destination later. Choosing HCMC in June inverts that. **[V]**
- **Stacking is allowed but discounted.** HKU permits other scholarships for the same
  programme *and* reserves the right to adjust subsidy **downwards** given other
  sponsorship. **[V]** So MicroBit's HK$5,000 is not additive to a full MES ceiling.

**Tung & Ngai.** The HK$80,000 exchange subsidy is a **shared cap** across one
compulsory Mainland plus optional international, duration flexible, per the primer's
reading. **Nothing published says LINK draws on that cap and I am not assuming it
does.** Whether a 3-week Horizons course satisfies the compulsory Mainland
requirement stays **[UNVERIFIED]** — only AASO/Foundation can answer.

**The collision that matters.** IAO publishes only that selected candidates are

ask about remote interviewing in the same email as the eligibility question.
Mid-January is also when Nansen historically opens NEWDAY admissions. A Shanghai

## E) NEWDAY DNA, and whether winter can match it

**NEWDAY. [V]** Nansen East-West Dialogue Academy, Lillehammer. **9 days, 29 Jul –
6 Aug 2026.** East Asian plus Nordic/European students; lectures, dialogue groups,
workshops, museum visits (Bjerkebæk, Aulestad), walks in the surrounding nature,
campfire evenings. **NOK 11,000** double / **NOK 15,000** single, including
programme, excursions, food and lodging; travel excluded. 2026 admissions ran "from
20th of January 2026 and onwards, until the course is full." Organisers include
**Daniel Bell (HKU)** and Chunrong Liu (Fudan-European Centre) — the HKU hook. Grew
out of the annual "Understanding Asia" course.

**NEWDAY 2027 is not published.** The Nansen course index lists ten courses; NEWDAY
is the only East-West dialogue product and its page still describes 2026. No 2027
dates, price, or form exists on the site. **[V]** A 2027 call surfacing around late
January 2027 is **[UNVERIFIED] inference** from the 2026 pattern, not a date. Nansen
publishes **no winter equivalent** — the other nine are art, art history, cyanotype,
writers' seminars, chamber music, an Olympic academy, WEXFO, and a Blå Kors course.
**[V]**

**So winter cannot buy NEWDAY**, only structural cousins, and the Recognized winter
list is thin on them:

- **Most European winter schools fall outside the window.** univie: winter school ran
  **30 Jan – 13 Feb 2026** in Vienna **[V]** — after Sem 2 starts and outside
  24 Dec – 17 Jan. A 2027 edition is unpublished.
- **In-window options are East Asian, area-studies shaped, not dialogic.** Sophia's
  January Session ran **7–28 Jan 2026** with an optional off-campus week to 4 Feb;
  2027 dates unpublished. **[V]** Fudan's winter session is **21 Dec 2026 – 8 Jan
  2027**, CNY 16,200 early-bird / 18,000 full. **[V]**

**Blunt read.** If you want the NEWDAY *form* — small, residential, dialogic, East
meets West, walks in the woods — winter 2026/27 is the wrong season to shop, and
LINK-S is not a substitute: LINK is a project-execution course with industry visits
and a demo day, assessed on delivery. Different genre. The coherent play is winter as
the **Mainland-and-delivery** chapter, NEWDAY as a **summer 2027** candidate — where
it then competes with Laidlaw LiA and China Vision summers, as the primer flags.
