# Charter

## What this corpus is for

1. **Maturity under transition** — Kissinger’s diagnostic questions as a recurring discipline.  
2. **Cohort readiness** — why join Tung & Ngai (or any leadership community) *now*, without flattery.  
3. **Path coherence** — sharpen Bob’s identity: craft, Mainland embed, comparison-travel, return to Hong Kong.  
4. **Diffusion of thought** — read beyond immediate utility so judgment has range.

## Inclusion test (both required)

A passage enters only if:

1. It could change a decision Bob may face (path relevance), **and**  
2. It illuminates a wider human / institutional / ethical problem (maturity relevance).

## Intellectual standards

- Prefer primary works and standard translations.  
- Separate **exact passage** from **plain reconstruction**.  
- Always record strongest objection and transfer limit.  
- Changing one’s mind is output, not failure.  
- No private vault material; no PII.

## Exclusions

- Quote collections used as personality costume.  
- Civilizational essences (“the East thinks…”) without period/institution.  
- National slogans as substitutes for observation.  
- Borrowed suffering as personal depth.  
- Invented or unverified quotations presented as exact.

## Relationship to Tung & Ngai

The Foundation’s scarce goods (cultural fluency, networks, sustained immersion) and soft rubric (優秀→卓越 · 專業→跨界 · 跟隨→創造) are **application contexts**, not the only lens. Thinkers are not Foundation mouthpieces.
