# Anti-patterns

| Anti-pattern | Failure | Correction |
|--------------|---------|------------|
| Quote salad | Prestige lines replace argument | One claim, one objection, one consequence |
| Civilizational grandiosity | Timeless “East/West/China” personality | Period, institution, group, mechanism, counterexample |
| China slogans | Formula replaces observation | Situated evidence; distinguish state / society / locality / generation |
| Identity theatre | Belonging performed for credit | State access, obligation, correction, or cost created |
| Airport cosmopolitanism | Polished impressions of visible difference | Ordinary systems; transfer barriers |
| Leadership self-coronation | Destiny narrative | Bounded responsibility, dependence, mistakes, output |
| Prestige bibliography | Names without changed judgment | Keep only what alters a question or decision |
| Borrowed certainty | Thinker’s confidence becomes yours | Conditional form + failure conditions |
| Borrowed suffering | Others’ history as depth prop | Preserve specificity; don’t claim unlived experience |
| Moralized sacrifice | Costs on others called noble | Who chooses, who pays, alternatives, limits |
| Retrospective neatness | Mess becomes flawless growth arc | Keep hesitation, luck, unresolved ends |
| Institutional cynicism | Incentives explain everything | Also record institutional goods and repair paths |
| False balance | Equal weight regardless of evidence | Fair objection, then judge |
| Identity as expertise | Proximity = knowledge | Separate experience / interpretation / research / verified claim |
