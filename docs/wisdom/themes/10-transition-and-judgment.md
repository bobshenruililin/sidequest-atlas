# 10 — Transition and judgment

**Path relevance:** Why join a leadership cohort *now*; how to think in contested futures (HK / China / GBA / public health).  
**Maturity reach:** Judgment when inherited yardsticks weaken.

---

## Kissinger — diagnostic leadership in transition

**Confidence: HIGH** (concordant Goodreads / reviews / secondary reprints of *Leadership: Six Studies in World Strategy*, 2022)

> Leadership is most essential during periods of transition, when values and institutions are losing their relevance, and the outlines of a worthy future are in controversy. In such times, leaders are called upon to think creatively and diagnostically: what are the sources of the society’s well-being? Of its decay? Which inheritances from the past should be preserved, and which adapted or discarded? Which objectives deserve commitment, and which prospects must be rejected no matter how tempting? And, at the extreme, is one’s society sufficiently vital and confident to tolerate sacrifice as a waystation to a more fulfilling future?

**Plain reconstruction:** Leadership’s highest use is not routine management but *diagnosis under contested futures* — inheritances, objectives, and whether sacrifice is bearable.

**Strongest objection:** Kissinger’s case studies are statecraft elites; moral costs of his own career are contested. Do not baptize geopolitical realpolitik as personal ethics.

**Transfer to you / T&N:** A scholar community is a miniature “society” under transition (career, China fluency, HK role). Ask his questions of *yourself* and of Hong Kong’s health systems — without claiming to be a statesman.

**Reflection prompts**
1. Name one inheritance in Hong Kong public health you would preserve; one you would adapt; one you would discard — with evidence, not vibe.  
2. What “tempting prospect” must you reject this year (prestige itinerary, Seam Ready as logo, identity theatre)?  
3. What sacrifice would joining T&N actually demand of you — time, ego, concurrent-award honesty, service depth?

## Arendt — thinking without banisters

**Confidence: HIGH** (Arendt, “Understanding and Politics,” *Essays in Understanding*; widely cited formulation of *Denken ohne Geländer*)

> Even though we have lost yardsticks by which to measure, and rules under which to subsume the particular, a being whose essence is a beginning may have enough of origin within himself to understand without preconceived categories and to judge without the set of customary rules which is morality.

**Plain reconstruction:** When tradition no longer supplies reliable measures, thinking and judging must still proceed — without borrowed certainty.

**Objection:** “Without banisters” can become aesthetic radicalism or excuse for ignoring hard-won norms.

**Transfer:** Cross-boundary health work often lacks a single moral handbook. Listening at WHS, then revising questions after Gansu, is practice of judgment — not a license to invent standards.

---

## Weber — vocation: passion and proportion

**Confidence: HIGH** (*Politik als Beruf*, 1919; standard English translations)

> Politics is a strong and slow boring of hard boards. It takes both passion and perspective.

> Only he who in the face of all this can say ‘In spite of all!’ has the calling for politics.

**Plain reconstruction:** Serious public work requires *Leidenschaft* (passion) fused with *Augenmaß* (sense of proportion) — and endurance when the world looks too stupid or base for what you offer.

**Objection:** Weber’s “politics” is state power; do not inflate student leadership into vocation-for-violence-and-bureaucracy.

**Transfer:** Heat-health briefs, Mainland coordination, cohort contribution — slow boring of hard boards. Passion without proportion = mobility collector. Proportion without passion = CV hygiene.

---

## Berlin — hedgehog and fox

**Confidence: HIGH** (Berlin citing Archilochus in *The Hedgehog and the Fox*)

> The fox knows many things, but the hedgehog knows one big thing.

**Plain reconstruction (Berlin’s use):** Some minds organize life around one central vision; others pursue many ends. Classification is a tool, not a destiny.

**Objection:** Overused as cocktail typology; Berlin himself treated it lightly.

**Transfer:** Your risk is fox-scattering (physics, weather, debate, WHS, Nordics, LatAm, Gansu…). Your hedgehog claim must stay narrow: *cross-boundary public health through Hong Kong*. Fox methods serve one big thing — or they are tourism.

## Heifetz — technical vs adaptive (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED** (concept from Ronald Heifetz, *Leadership Without Easy Answers* — paraphrase, not a single famous sentence)

**Reconstruction:** *Technical* problems have known solutions experts can apply; *adaptive* challenges require people to change priorities, loyalties, and learning — authority alone cannot download the answer.

**Transfer:** Laidlaw methods are partly technical; trust across the HK–Mainland seam is adaptive. T&N’s value is less tuition than forced adaptive learning with peers.

---

## Hirschman — possibilism (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED** (Albert O. Hirschman, possibilism vs “fracasomania”)

**Reconstruction:** Reform fails when we demand total solutions; progress often comes from sequential, partial, opportunistic moves that respect unintended consequences.

**Transfer:** Seam Ready as total interoperability fantasy fails; smallest useful test with a partner succeeds. Kissinger’s “reject tempting prospects” pairs with Hirschman’s anti-grandiosity.

---

## Synthesis for this theme

Joining a leadership cohort in transition is rational when you need **shared diagnostic practice** — people who will force preserve/adapt/discard questions — not when you need a brand. Kissinger supplies the questions; Arendt warns against borrowed yardsticks; Weber demands passion-with-proportion; Berlin warns against fox-scatter; Heifetz/Hirschman keep ambition implementable.
