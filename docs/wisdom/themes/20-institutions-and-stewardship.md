# 20 — Institutions and stewardship

**Path relevance:** CHP / China CDC / HA / universities / T&N as institutions — not abstractions.  
**Maturity reach:** Reform without romance; loyalty without capture.

---

## Selznick — institutional character (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED** (Philip Selznick, *Leadership in Administration*)

**Reconstruction:** Organizations become *institutions* when they are infused with value beyond bare technical tasks; leadership is stewardship of that character against co-optation and drift.

**Transfer:** T&N’s “lifelong community” claim is institutional character. Your job if awarded: protect it by returning artifacts, not extracting mentors.

---

## Scott — legibility

**Confidence: MED–HIGH** (James C. Scott, *Seeing Like a State* — thesis widely attested; verify page before public cite)

**Reconstruction:** States (and large systems) make society *legible* through simplification — maps, categories, metrics. That enables governance and often destroys local knowledge (*métis*) that made the system work.

**Objection:** Anti-state romanticism can ignore public goods that require legibility (surveillance, vaccine campaigns).

**Transfer:** Heat–CV admissions are HA-legible data. Gansu temple-medical and three bags of seeds are local knowledge. Cross-boundary work fails if you only speak one language — metrics *or* stories.

---

## Ostrom — governing the commons (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED** (Elinor Ostrom)

**Reconstruction:** Shared resources can be governed without pure privatization or pure centralization when users craft rules, monitor, and sanction with local knowledge.

**Transfer:** Scholar communities, open research tools, and cross-boundary data-sharing are commons problems. “Return usable things” needs rules, not vibes.

---

## Lipsky — street-level bureaucracy (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** Policy is remade by frontline workers under scarcity; discretion is where ideals meet triage.

**Transfer:** Rural health/infra service and hospital surge planning both live at street level. Listen to implementers before proposing interoperability.

---

## Jacobs — eyes on the street (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** Order often emerges from ordinary dense interactions, not master plans alone.

**Transfer:** Ordinary life as destination (Atlas philosophy) is an institutional epistemology: watch how people actually use clinics, ferries, markets.
