# 30 — Craft, vocation, and standards

**Path relevance:** “High-craft” must mean standards, not aesthetic branding.  
**Maturity reach:** Excellence for its own sake under prestige pressure.

---

## Sennett — craftsmanship

**Confidence: HIGH** (*The Craftsman*; “making is thinking” / “desire to do a job well for its own sake” widely reported from the book)

> Craftsmanship [is] the desire to do a job well for its own sake.

**Guiding intuition (reported):** making is thinking.

**Plain reconstruction:** Skill is a form of thought; quality is an ethical stance against pure efficiency theatre.

**Objection:** Craft romanticism can ignore unjust conditions of labor.

**Transfer:** A heat-health brief someone at HA/CHP could use is craft. A conference selfie is not. Laureate ambassadorship is craft only when access is stewarded well.

---

## MacIntyre — practices and internal goods (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED** (*After Virtue*)

**Reconstruction:** A *practice* has internal goods (excellence recognizable only inside the activity) and external goods (money, status). Institutions distribute external goods and can corrupt practices.

**Transfer:** Scholarship / epidemiology / coordination are practices. Awards are external goods. T&N money should enable internal goods — cultural fluency, better research — not replace them.

---

## Polanyi — tacit knowledge (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** We know more than we can tell; apprenticeship transmits skill that manuals cannot.

**Transfer:** Gansu twice > one brochure on “China.” Peer correction in labs > solitary brilliance.

---

## Schön — reflection-in-action (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** Professionals learn by reflecting while acting on unique cases that resist routine technique.

**Transfer:** Interview and field stories should show revision mid-action — not retrospective perfection.

---

## Gawande — fallibility and checklists (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** Complex work needs humility, teams, and disciplined safeguards — not heroics alone.

**Transfer:** Cross-boundary health is complex. Your “research before service” is a checklist ethic; still need partners.

---

## Confucius — learning and thought

**Confidence: HIGH** (*Analects* 为政; standard text)

> 学而不思则罔，思而不学则殆。  
> Learning without thought is labor lost; thought without learning is perilous.  
> (Legge tr.; confirm preferred translation)

**Transfer:** Laidlaw without field listening = perilous abstraction. Gansu without study = labor lost.
