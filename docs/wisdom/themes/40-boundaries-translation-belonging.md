# 40 — Boundaries, translation, belonging

**Path relevance:** Professional / cultural / institutional crossing is the identity spine.  
**Maturity reach:** Plural belonging without identity theatre.

---

## Appiah — rooted cosmopolitanism (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED** (*Cosmopolitanism*)

**Reconstruction:** We have obligations that stretch beyond our tribe, yet rooted partiality is legitimate; conversation across difference is the method.

**Transfer:** “Comparison not escape” is Appiah’s cosmopolitanism with a return address — Hong Kong.

---

## Said — representation and position (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** Descriptions of others are never neutral; intellectuals must watch how representation reproduces power.

**Objection:** Can become paralysis or purity contests.

**Transfer:** When you speak about Mainland rural life or Global South mobilisation, name your position and limits. Do not aestheticize Gansu.

---

## Du Bois — double consciousness (paraphrase / analogy caution)

**Confidence: MED** (concept from *The Souls of Black Folk* — historical specificity required)

**Reconstruction:** Seeing oneself through the eyes of a dominant other creates a split perspective that can be painful and analytically sharp.

**Transfer limit:** Do **not** equate Bob’s soft identity navigation with African American historical experience. Use only the *method*: awareness of multiple audiences without performing for them.

---

## Fei Xiaotong — differential mode of association (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED** (*Xiangtu Zhongguo* / *From the Soil*)

**Reconstruction:** Chinese social relations often radiate from the self through graded ties — not Western-style bounded group membership alone.

**Transfer:** WZQ coordination, mentorship debts, scholar communities — understand relational obligation without reducing China to timeless essence.

---

## Pratt — contact zones (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** Contact zones are social spaces where cultures meet under asymmetric power; people represent themselves under unequal conditions.

**Transfer:** Laureate Forum, WHS, Gansu, Medellín are contact zones. Ask: who sets the terms of speaking?
