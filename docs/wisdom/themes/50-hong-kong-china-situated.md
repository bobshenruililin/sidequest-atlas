## Mencius — people as weightiest

**Confidence: HIGH** (*Mencius* 尽心下; Chinese Text Project)

> 民为贵，社稷次之，君为轻。  
> The people are the most important; the altars of state come next; the ruler is lightest.  
> (standard sense; Legge/ctext variants exist)

**Plain reconstruction:** Legitimacy ranks the people’s welfare above symbols and rulers.

**Objection:** Classical minben is not modern democracy; do not anachronize.

**Transfer:** Public health returns to *people’s* exposure and trust — not prestige of CDC names. Heat harming elderly admissions is a 民为贵 test.

---

## Shangshu tradition — people as root (related)

**Confidence: HIGH** as classical formula (《尚书》 “民惟邦本，本固邦宁” — widely attested)

> 民惟邦本，本固邦宁。  
> The people are the root of the state; when the root is firm, the state is tranquil.

**Transfer:** Cultural confidence can mean serious study of this ethic in practice — rural listening, urban heat preparedness — not slogan recital.

## Wang Gungwu — Chinese worlds / migration (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** “Chineseness” is historically plural — merchant networks, diaspora, state forms — not one essence.

**Transfer:** Soft identity for you: enact China/HK commitment through work; avoid birthplace drama; think historically about mobility.

## Kissinger on Lee Kuan Yew — strategy of excellence (framing)

**Confidence: MED** (Kissinger’s *Leadership* framing of LKY — secondary)

**Reconstruction:** Kissinger presents LKY as building a city-state through disciplined excellence amid vulnerability.

**Objection:** Singapore is not Hong Kong; authoritarian costs matter; do not cosplay LKY.

**Transfer:** Excellence + vulnerability + institutions — useful *questions*, not a model to copy.

## Anti-slogan discipline (corpus rule)

When speaking of China’s rise / GBA / cultural confidence:

1. Name a **period** and **institution**.  
2. Name a **mechanism** (surveillance, hospital surge, data standard…).  
3. Name a **counterexample** or uncertainty.  
4. Name a **Hong Kong return**.

If you cannot, you are sloganizing.
