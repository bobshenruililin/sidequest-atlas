## Tocqueville — seeing institutions through travel

**Confidence: MED–HIGH** (*Democracy in America* — method more than one line)

**Reconstruction:** Tocqueville used America to understand democracy’s habits, associations, and soft despotism risks — comparative observation as political education.

**Famous soft-despotism strain (paraphrase, confirm edition):** a tutelary power that provides for citizens’ needs until they lose the uses of themselves.

**Transfer:** Nordics high-trust systems are a Tocquevillean mirror for HK — ask what associations and habits produce trust, what cannot import.
