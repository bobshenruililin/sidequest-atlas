# 70 — Scholarship, community, and return

**Path relevance:** T&N lifelong community; “returns something usable.”  
**Maturity reach:** Knowledge as obligation, not extraction.

---

## Dewey — inquiry as associated life (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** Democracy is associated living; education reconstructs experience; inquiry solves public problems through consequences.

**Transfer:** Scholar forums and salons should be Deweyan — shared problem-solving — not networking theatre.

---

## Addams — reciprocal inquiry (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** Settlement work corrects the investigator; proximity disciplines theory; service without reciprocity becomes paternalism.

**Transfer:** WZQ / Dandelion-type service: who corrects you? What did local partners find useful?

---

## Freire — dialogue vs banking (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** Education as dialogue and praxis, not depositing answers into empty accounts.

**Transfer:** Peer teaching and climate-health literacy pods — dialogue. Lecture tourism — banking.

---

## Wenger — communities of practice (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** Learning happens through participation in shared practice with common repertoire.

**Transfer:** T&N cohort as community of practice only if there is shared work (projects, critique), not shared brand.

---

## Mandela — shepherd leadership

**Confidence: HIGH** for autobiography wording (*Long Walk to Freedom*); **LOW** for meme “lead from the back and let others believe…”

> A leader, he said, is like a shepherd. He stays behind the flock, letting the most nimble go out ahead, whereupon the others follow, not realizing that all along they are being directed from behind.

**Transfer:** Cohort contribution = making others capable; Laureate hosting = shepherding access. Do not use meme paraphrase in public writing.

---

## Boyer — scholarship of engagement (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** Scholarship includes discovery, integration, application, teaching — engagement is not lesser.

**Transfer:** Policy brief + peer workshop = engaged scholarship; prestige paper alone is incomplete for your identity.
