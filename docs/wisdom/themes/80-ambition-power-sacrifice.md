# 80 — Ambition, power, and sacrifice

**Path relevance:** Kissinger’s sacrifice question; Foundation money and prestige.  
**Maturity reach:** Desire without self-deception.

---

## Weber — ethic of responsibility vs conviction (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED–HIGH** (*Politics as a Vocation*)

**Reconstruction:** Ethic of conviction follows pure intention; ethic of responsibility owns foreseeable consequences. Mature vocation needs both — especially responsibility for outcomes.

**Transfer:** “I meant well” does not excuse bad heat-health advice or overclaimed Mainland impact.

---

## Niebuhr — moral man and immoral society (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** Groups concentrate power and self-interest more stubbornly than individuals; moral idealism must reckon with collective egoism.

**Transfer:** Institutions (universities, foundations, states) are not friendship circles. Enter with clear eyes.

---

## Camus — limits of rebellion (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED** (*The Rebel*)

**Reconstruction:** Rebellion affirms a shared human limit; it refuses to justify cruelty by a promised future.

**Transfer:** Reject “necessary” harm to communities for your narrative arc. Sacrifice of *others* is not your nobility.

---

## Murdoch — attention and fantasy (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** Moral life is largely a matter of attention; the ego fantasizes; seeing others clearly is hard work.

**Transfer:** Soft identity care is Murdochian: don’t let biography fantasy eat the interview. Attend to the panel’s actual questions.

---

## Weil — attention as generosity (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** Attention to the afflicted is a rare form of generosity; thought can refuse to turn suffering into spectacle.

**Transfer:** Gansu stories — attend, don’t perform.

---

## Girard — mimetic desire (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** We desire what others desire; prestige rivalry produces scapegoats.

**Transfer:** Cohort envy, award competition, conference FOMO — name the mimesis. Desire the *work*, not the mirror of rivals.

---

## Mencius — adversity and ease

**Confidence: HIGH** (common Mencius saying; verify chapter before public cite)

> 生于忧患，死于安乐。  
> We survive through adversity and perish through ease.  
> (sense; confirm exact locus)

**Transfer:** Financial comfort from awards can soften craft. Use ease to deepen work, not to collect stamps.
