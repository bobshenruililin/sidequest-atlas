# 90 — Self-command, friendship, revision

**Path relevance:** Who can correct Bob; how identity stays soft and honest.  
**Maturity reach:** Private honesty under public ambition.

---

## Montaigne — self-scrutiny (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** Essays as self-examination; fallibility as method; friendship as rare equality.

**Transfer:** Journals / Atlas captures should be Montaignian — revision visible — not PR.

---

## Epictetus — what is up to us (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** Distinguish what is in your control from what is not; do not waste the soul on the latter.

**Objection:** Stoicism can excuse structural injustice.

**Transfer:** Control: preparation, honesty of tenses, artifact shipping. Not control: panel seats, CDC offers, invitation optics.

---

## Zhuangzi — perspective and skill (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** Rigid categories trap; skilled responsiveness (*you*) moves with the grain of things; status distinctions are comic from the Way’s view.

**Transfer:** Release “must look like a T&N scholar.” Do the work that fits the problem.

---

## Confucius — friendship and correction

**Confidence: HIGH** (*Analects* themes; e.g. 友直、友谅、友多闻)

**Reconstruction:** Beneficial friends are upright, forgiving, and learned; harmful friends are crooked, soft-mouthed, and flashy-tongued. (Confirm preferred chapter/translation.)

**Transfer:** Choose cohort peers who will correct you — not only celebrate you.

---

## Nussbaum — narrative imagination (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** Emotions can be forms of judgment; narrative imagination enlarges moral perception of others’ lives.

**Transfer:** Use carefully — imagination is not evidence. Pair with field correction.

---

## Midgley — mixed motives (paraphrase)

**Confidence: MED**

**Reconstruction:** Humans are not single-drive machines; conceptual confusion produces bad ethics.

**Transfer:** Admit mixed motives for awards (need, ambition, service, belonging). Honesty > purity cosplay.

---

## Reflection prompts

1. Name one person who can tell you “this is prestige theatre” — and be heard.  
2. What belief about your path would you drop if a partner said the work was useless?  
3. Where are your motives mixed — and how do you govern them without self-loathing or self-flattery?
