# GLOCAL Connect — mentee welcome messages (Aug 2026)

**Use:** first contact with assigned new non-local undergraduates, group setup, and follow-up.
**Drafted:** 2026-08-17 (Bob in Helsinki; HKT = Helsinki + 5 in August)
**Voice:** `docs/writing-voice/STYLE.md` — messaging register, so shorter sentences than the essay corpus, but the same rule: no brochure diction, no invented facts.

## Privacy note

The roster (full names, halls, phone numbers, WeChat IDs, home cities) stays **out of this repository**.
This file holds templates and slot variables only. Fill `[name]` / `[hall]` / `[home]` from the CEDARS
list at send time. Never paste the roster, a screenshot of it, or anyone's number into the group chat.

## Fact discipline

Do not assert hall rules, move-in dates, fees, orientation schedules, canteen hours, SIM prices, or
transport fares in any of these messages. Offer to *find* the official answer instead.

Lived facts that are fair to use: Bob is Year 2 GHD, St John's, and an HKUMed peer mentor through
this scheme. Do not turn a Colombia mentee into a case study, and do not invent hall gossip.

## 1. Group name

GLOCAL = **glo**bal + lo**cal**. The extra **L** is the point: you already brought the globe; this chat is the local half.

| Option | Note |
|--------|------|
| **gloCAL crew** | Favourite. The caps do the joke: CAL = local. Short enough for a chat list. |
| **the extra L** | Cleaner, more Bob. People will ask what it means, which is useful. Add `GLOCAL` in the description so CEDARS can still find it. |
| **GLOCAL (not a typo)** | Safest if you want the scheme name visible in notifications. |

Do **not** use: *Go Local lol*, *Glo-gal*, hashtag piles, or a name that only works if you already know the pun.

**Description (one line):** *Not a typo for GLOBAL. The extra L is local — buses, bedding, food.*

In the 1:1, one light mention is enough. Do not pun in all seven DMs.

## 6. Send timing while in Helsinki

August 2026: Helsinki UTC+3, Hong Kong UTC+8, so **HK is Helsinki + 5**.

| Recipient's likely location | Send window (Bob's local Helsinki time) |
|---|---|
| Already in Hong Kong / Mainland China | 09:00–15:00 → lands 14:00–20:00 HKT |
| Australia (UTC+10 in August, no DST) | 06:00–13:00 |
| UK (UTC+1) | 12:00–21:00 |
| Italy (UTC+2) | 11:00–21:00 |
| Colombia (UTC-5) and Canada (UTC-4 to -7) | 18:00–22:00 |

A phone number's country code says where the SIM was bought, not where the person is. When in doubt,
default to Hong Kong daytime and keep the "no need to reply quickly" line in. Avoid sending anything
after 17:00 Helsinki to HK-based mentees; that's past 22:00 for them.

**If it is late evening in Helsinki:** message Canada / Colombia / UK numbers now. Hold Mainland China,
Hong Kong, and uncertain Asia numbers until 09:00–15:00 Helsinki the next day.

## 7. What not to do

- Don't add anyone to the group before they say yes.
- Don't post numbers, WeChat IDs, or the roster in the chat; don't let the group become a directory.
- Don't state hall rules, move-in dates, fees, orientation timings, or fares you haven't verified.
  "I'll check and send you the official page" is a better answer than a confident wrong one.
- Don't single out the GHD mentee in the group. Shared-programme energy belongs in her own chat.
- Don't write like an office: no "Dear all", no "I hope this message finds you well", no
  "welcome to the HKU family".
- No all-caps, no hashtag walls, no emoji confetti. One emoji at most, if any.
- Don't double- or triple-text into silence. One follow-up, then email, then stop.
- Don't open with a voice note or a call. Text first; ask before calling.
- Don't discuss anyone's visa, money, health, or family situation in the group.
- Don't share the group invite link anywhere public.
- Don't rename the group repeatedly or run admin theatrics. Set it once, then be useful.
- Don't promise to be online constantly while travelling. Say when you're back and keep to it.
