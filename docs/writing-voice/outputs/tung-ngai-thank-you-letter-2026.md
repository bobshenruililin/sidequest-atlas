Dear Professor Wong,

Thank you for awarding me the Tung and Ngai Foundation Scholarship. I am deeply grateful to the Tung Chao-Yu & Ngai Chue Jai Lily Education Foundation, and to its founders, Mr Tung Chao-Yu and Mdm Ngai Chue-Jai Lily, for this generous support.

The Scholarship means that I can give my full attention to my studies. The tuition and living allowance take a real weight off my family, and the exchange subsidy allows the compulsory Mainland programme to be a proper working stay, time spent learning how a community and its health system work, rather than a brief visit.

My goal is a public-health career at the interface between Hong Kong and the Mainland. During my studies I hope to complete a field placement with the China CDC, and after graduation to work at the Centre for Health Protection in Hong Kong. If permitted, I would put the Endeavour Support Fund towards this public-health path in the Greater Bay Area and the Mainland.

The Foundation's commitment to service is one I share, and this summer I tried to act on it. Through HKU Horizons and the Wu Zhi Qiao (Bridge to China) Charitable Foundation, I spent two weeks of service-learning in Macha Village, Gansu, as a student coordinator, after a study visit to Shaanxi. In small groups we studied how a village on dry loess land manages its water, how rammed-earth houses are built and repaired, and how families make a living; we also worked with our hands on village infrastructure, including installing solar street lamps. At the Nansen East-West Dialogue Academy in Lillehammer, Norway, I joined a conversation with the peace mediator Steinar Bryn on what it means to serve. Two photographs from the summer are attached.

The scheme asks scholars to mentor those who come after, and I will. I also plan to keep serving in Hong Kong, and to treat my time on the Mainland as service in its own right. A gift made for people the founders would never meet is repaid in work. I intend to do the work.

Yours sincerely,

SHEN Ruililin  
BASc Global Health and Development  
The University of Hong Kong
