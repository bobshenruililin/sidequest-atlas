# Youde local — nomination email (send this week)

**Scheme:** Sir Edward Youde Memorial Scholarships (for Undergraduate and Diploma Students) — **local UG**, not overseas.  
**Why this email exists:** Year 2+ is **institution nomination**. You cannot apply on Portal. WFSFAA: heads of institutions are invited **August / September**; interviews **late November / December**; if no interview invite by **end of December**, you were not selected.  
**Last published value:** HK$40,000 one-off for **2025/26**. 2026/27 amount unpublished. The Fund says other scholarships **need not be given up** and Youde **will not be reduced** — still read the Tung & Ngai letter before you treat a win as spendable.  
**Not this:** Youde **overseas** fellowships (AAS board: **4 Sep 2026**). Do not mix them.

Send from your **HKU address**. Fill `[UID]`. Confirm CGPA in SIS before you hit send. Do not attach HKID.

---

## Headers

**To:** `scholarships@hku.hk`  
**Cc:** `ghdsph@hku.hk`  
**Subject:** Nomination enquiry — Sir Edward Youde Memorial Scholarships (local undergraduate) — Shen Ruililin, BASc(GHD) Year 2

## Body (paste)

Dear Academic Advising and Scholarships Office,

I am writing to ask whether the University will nominate students this cycle for the **Sir Edward Youde Memorial Scholarships (for Undergraduate and Diploma Students)** — the **local** undergraduate scheme — and, if so, whether I might be considered.

I am Shen Ruililin, UID `[UID]`, a Year 2 student in the **BASc(Global Health and Development)** programme and a Hong Kong permanent resident. My current CGPA is **4.09 / 4.30**. I am not in full-time employment. I understand that for Year 2 and above the Fund invites heads of institutions to nominate, usually in August or September, and that students do not apply directly.

I am asking because the work I want to do is local in the demanding sense. Last spring, on an HKU Horizons visit to Gannan, I watched a woman measure a road with her body and a caretaker name his dog Buddha. I came home hungry, not finished. That is why I spent May and June on Wu Zhi Qiao in Macha, and why my Laidlaw research with Professor David Bishai is on **heat and older adults in Hong Kong** — not as a conference topic, but as a seam: weather, ageing, and whether two health systems can still trust each other’s numbers. GHD is the degree I chose for that seam. I intend to stay in it.

I already hold a Tung & Ngai Foundation Scholarship and a Laidlaw Scholarship. I am writing to Youde anyway because WFSFAA states that Youde scholars need not relinquish other awards, and because Youde is one of the few Hong Kong names that still means **academic seriousness in service of this place**. I would rather be nominated honestly, with those awards on the table, than omit them. If a nomination would conflict with University or Foundation rules, I will of course follow the letters.

I would be grateful to know:

1. whether HKU is nominating for the **local** Youde undergraduate scholarships this cycle;  
2. the internal deadline and any forms;  
3. whether a Year 2 GHD student may be put forward, and what you need from me (transcript, CV, referee).

I have copied the GHD programme office (`ghdsph@hku.hk`). I can send an unofficial transcript and a one-page CV immediately.

Thank you for your time — and for the unglamorous work of matching students to the right door.

Yours sincerely,  
**Shen Ruililin**  
Year 2, BASc(Global Health and Development)  
The University of Hong Kong  
UID: `[UID]`

Then keep the numbered questions and the close.

---

## Do not

- Write the **overseas** Youde office or use the 4 September deadline.  
- Claim a 2026/27 dollar amount.  
- Perform poverty. Youde local is merit.  
- Hide Tung & Ngai.  
- CC the Fund secretariat; they do not take student self-nominations for this scheme.  
- Send HKID, award-letter scans, or a five-page CV with the first email.

## After you send

If AAS replies “faculty nominate first,” forward the same text to GHD and ask the programme office to endorse. If nobody replies in **five working days**, one polite bump to the same thread. Then stop.
